Three markets, three stories — and only one can be right

Oil down 9.4%, tech up 20% in 20 days, and the 30-year stuck near 5% — three contradictory signals, and the bond market is the honest arbiter.

This week's markets delivered irreconcilable signals. Crude futures collapsed nearly 12% over the past month, pricing demand destruction. Technology stocks surged roughly 20%, pricing sustained growth. Meanwhile, the 30-year Treasury yield refused to break below 5%, despite an oil shock that would normally fuel a bond rally. The short end of the curve steepened further, with the 3-month to 10-year spread widening to its best reading of the cycle.

One of these three cannot be true simultaneously. Something has to give: either the demand pessimism in energy, the growth confidence in equities, or the fiscal and supply constraints keeping long rates pinned higher. Which market is right will determine whether we're approaching growth or contraction.
