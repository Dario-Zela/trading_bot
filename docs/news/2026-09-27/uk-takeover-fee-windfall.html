UK takeover frenzy nets bankers and lawyers a £600m windfall

In one sentence
Over £60bn in bids for London-listed firms including Easyjet, Tate & Lyle and Schroders has generated record advisory fees, fuelling concerns the market is becoming a consolidation target rather than a growth venue.
