Man City agree Rulli deal as Liverpool close in on Araujo loan

Gerónimo Rulli, pictured playing for Argentina, has agreed a two-year deal to return to Manchester City from Marseille. — Wikimedia Commons
In one sentence
Man City have agreed a deal to sign Marseille goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli as Liverpool near a season-long loan for Barcelona's Ronald Araújo.

Manchester City have agreed a deal to bring back a familiar face between the posts, striking terms with Marseille for Argentina goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli, while across the Pennines-and-M62 divide Liverpool are closing in on a season-long loan for Barcelona centre-back Ronald Araújo. Both moves land in the same frantic week of the window, and both are, in their own way, about clubs papering over gaps left by summer departures.

In one sentence: Man City have agreed a deal to sign Marseille goalkeeper Gerónimo Rulli as Liverpool near a season-long loan for Barcelona's Ronald Araújo.

City's second homecoming for Rulli

Rulli, 34, will undergo a medical at the Etihad on Monday after City and Marseille settled on a fee reported between €3.5m and €3.8m — a modest outlay for a goalkeeper with a 2022 World Cup winner's medal and a Europa League final penalty save (for Villarreal, against Manchester United in 2021) on his résumé. He signs a two-year contract, returning to a club he first joined in 2016 and left without making a senior appearance, before a decade spent building a career at Real Sociedad, Villarreal, Ajax and Marseille.

The move fills the hole left by James Trafford's departure to Leeds United, who paid a club-record fee reported at around £40m plus add-ons to make the 23-year-old England international the most expensive British goalkeeper in history. Director of football Hugo Viana had identified Rulli as his preferred option to understudy Gianluigi Donnarumma, City's first choice since Trafford struggled to dislodge him last season. It is a low-risk, low-cost solution to a problem City created themselves by cashing in on their academy product — pension off some of the transfer windfall from Trafford into a keeper who needs no bedding-in period and will start the season, in all likelihood, on the bench.

Liverpool move for Araújo as defensive options thin

Liverpool's business points the same direction — shoring up a position stretched thin by exits — but at centre-back rather than in goal. Ronald Araújo, 27, is set to join on a season-long loan, with Barcelona director Deco understood to have signed off on the move and reporters Fabrizio Romano and Matteo Moretto both confirming an agreement is close.

The Uruguayan, who captains Barcelona and made over 200 appearances since arriving in 2018, has slipped behind Gerard Martín, Pau Cubarsí and Jules Koundé in Hansi Flick's pecking order, starting only 11 LaLiga games last season after an extended break from the game in November 2025 while he dealt with depression. A permanent stay looked unlikely regardless — Barcelona handed him a contract through 2031 only last year, underlining that this is squad management rather than a player being pushed out the door.

For Liverpool, the need is more pressing. Ibrahima Konaté left for Real Madrid on a free transfer at the end of last season, Joe Gomez picked up a muscle injury in pre-season, and new signing Jeremy Jacquet, just 21, lacks senior experience. Giovanni Leoni is still working back from an ACL injury. That leaves Virgil van Dijk as, by some distance, the only fully fit senior centre-back at Arne Slot's disposal heading into the new campaign — hardly the position a title-chasing squad wants to be in three weeks before a ball is kicked in anger.

Both deals still need the paperwork to catch up with the reporting: Rulli's medical is pencilled in for Monday, and Liverpool's loan has yet to be formally announced by either club. Expect confirmation from both sides before the transfer window closes on 1 September, with City's goalkeeping business likely done and Liverpool still expected to be active at the back.
