Hot US inflation pushes the 10-year Treasury yield to 5% and hits Nvidia, as Fed officials talk tougher

A hot US inflation print reportedly pushed the 10-year yield to 5% and knocked Nvidia down about 6%, while the wider chip rally held and Fed officials sounded tougher.

The move hit Nvidia hardest: its shares fell about 6% in the same session, while the broader semiconductor rally has so far held. Higher yields raise the cost of borrowing across the economy, from mortgages to corporate debt, and they weigh most on richly valued growth stocks whose earnings lie far in the future.

The selloff was concentrated rather than sector-wide. Investors appear to be treating Nvidia's drop as a stock-specific reaction to rates, not a verdict on chipmakers as a group. Fed officials, meanwhile, have taken a firmer tone on inflation, which lifts the odds that policy stays restrictive for longer.
