Noskova beats Gauff to reach WTA Finals semi-finals; England squad news on McTominay and Pickford

In one sentence
Linda Noskova beat Coco Gauff 6-3, 6-3 to reach the WTA Finals semi-finals; the England squad items in the cluster could not be verified.

Linda Noskova has made the last four of the WTA Finals at her first attempt, and she did it by taking Coco Gauff apart. The Czech won 6-3, 6-3 on Tuesday in their Group 1 match, according to Reuters, and with it she is through to the semi-finals.

A serve that went missing

Gauff's serve decided it. She won only 43% of points behind her first serve in the opening set, and Noskova broke her five times over the match, four of them in that first set. Both players served nine-ish aces' worth of power — Noskova hit nine, Gauff eight — but Gauff also double-faulted eight times to Noskova's five. Against a hard-hitting opponent in a sprint of a round-robin, that is a costly ledger.

The result was Noskova's second in two group matches. She had already beaten Kostyuk in her opening match, and she has yet to drop a set. Two wins from two guarantees her a place in the knockout stage.

What is still open

Gauff, on one win and one defeat, is not out. The final round-robin matches on Thursday will settle who joins Noskova from the group, and the standings elsewhere in the draw, including Pegula's chances, are still moving. For Noskova, the remaining group match matters mainly for seeding into the semi-finals, since she cannot be caught for qualification.

It is a notable result for two players in the top tier of the women's game, and a first semi-final at this event for Noskova. It does not change rankings or the structure of the tour, which is why it sits here as a brief.

A note on the England items

The other half of this cluster does not stand up. Scott McTominay's heart treatment was a pulsed field ablation for a mild arrhythmia, carried out in September while he was at Napoli, and it was widely reported at the time. He plays for Scotland, not England. A report linking his treatment to an England camp, or to Jordan Pickford's goalkeeping competition, could not be confirmed against any source, so neither is reported here as fact.

What to watch: Thursday's final group matches, which determine Gauff's fate and the semi-final pairings.
