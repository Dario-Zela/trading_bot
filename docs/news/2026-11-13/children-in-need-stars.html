BBC Children in Need draws Timothée Chalamet and other stars

In one sentence
The BBC's Children in Need appeal airs tonight with Alex Jones and Rylan Clark presenting, but Timothée Chalamet's involvement is unconfirmed; last year raised £45.5m.

The BBC's annual Children in Need appeal is on air tonight, and the billing promises famous faces. The headline name is Timothée Chalamet. The evidence for his involvement is thin. A search of BBC and wider coverage turned up no listing, statement or report placing him in the 2026 show. His name is best treated as unconfirmed until the BBC says otherwise.

What is confirmed

The 2026 appeal show airs on 13 November on BBC One and iPlayer. Alex Jones is the first presenter named for the team. Rylan Clark has also said he is joining as a presenter. More names were promised in the run-up to the broadcast.

The format is familiar. Children in Need is a telethon, usually held in mid-November, in which presenters, sketches and musical turns frame a running total of money raised. The BBC runs the broadcast. Money goes out through the Children in Need charity.

The appeal dates from 1980. Pudsey Bear, the yellow mascot with the spotted eyepatch, has been its public face since 1985. For many viewers the show is as much a seasonal ritual as a fundraiser.

The money

The benchmark is last year. The 2025 appeal raised £45.5m, according to the charity's own announcement. That figure is the one tonight's total will be measured against. The final tally is normally revealed at the end of the broadcast and then revised upward in the following weeks as donations and events are counted.

The Chalamet question

Celebrity cameos are a staple of the night, and a Hollywood name would be a coup for a UK charity broadcast. But the claim rests on a single headline from one source, and nothing found in the BBC's own announcements backs it. It could be a pre-recorded message, a sketch or a promotional tie-in, or it could be a misreading of the line-up. The article cannot say which.

The test is simple. The BBC's published schedule and its post-show coverage will show who appeared. If Chalamet is not among them, the headline was over-eager.

What to watch

The running total on the night, and the final figure the charity publishes in the days after, against last year's £45.5m. The BBC's post-broadcast line-up will settle the guest list.
