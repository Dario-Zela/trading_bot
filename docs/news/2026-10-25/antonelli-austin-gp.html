Antonelli close to a landmark result at the Austin Grand Prix

In one sentence
Antonelli starts the US Grand Prix on pole and could become F1's youngest champion at 20 years 61 days, but only if he wins and Russell and Hamilton both finish well down.

The Mercedes driver who was a rookie last year could become Formula 1's youngest world champion in Texas on Sunday. Andrea Kimi Antonelli, 20, starts the United States Grand Prix from pole position at the Circuit of the Americas. It is his first chance to wrap up the 2026 title with six races still to run.

The 'landmark' is the championship itself, and the age record that goes with it. Antonelli was born on 25 August 2006, so he would be 20 years and 61 days old on race day. The previous youngest champion is Sebastian Vettel, who won the 2010 title at 23.

What he needs

The arithmetic is stricter than a pole position suggests. After Singapore, Antonelli led the standings on 340 points. His team-mate George Russell had 238, Lewis Hamilton 229, Max Verstappen 221, Charles Leclerc 215 and Lando Norris 201.

According to Grandprix.com's analysis, Antonelli clinches in Austin only if three things happen together:

  • He wins the race.
  • Russell finishes ninth or lower.
  • Hamilton finishes fifth or lower.

The target is a 125-point lead after Austin. Six rounds offer a maximum of 150 points each, and a rival who trailed by less than 125 could still draw level. Any tie would go to Antonelli, though, because the tie-break counts race wins. He has eight this season to Russell's three, and a Texas win would make nine. A win would also remove Verstappen, Leclerc and Norris from the equation, since their best finish behind him would be second.

A second place would not be enough even if Russell failed to score. Misses on any of the three conditions only postpone the decision, and the next rounds are Mexico City on 1 November, then São Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi on 6 December.

Saturday's pole

Antonelli beat Verstappen to pole by 0.179 seconds, with a lap of 1:34.035. Isack Hadjar was third, Leclerc fourth and Hamilton fifth. Reports differ on whether this was his seventh or eighth pole of the year, the discrepancy probably being a sprint pole. Verstappen has won the last two grands prix and starts alongside him on the front row.

Pole is only part of the job at a circuit where the run to Turn 1 is steep and crowded. Hamilton, in fifth, is the driver whose finish matters most to the clinching maths after Russell's.

A label that has expired

The 'rookie' tag is out of date. Antonelli made his debut with Mercedes in 2025 and is in his second season. A title this early would end the debate about whether his results reflect the car or the driver. Mercedes' own garage also holds the nearest challenger in Russell.

What next

The race is 56 laps, and the result will show whether the title is settled or moves to Mexico City. The sources available before the race were previews. This article does not report the outcome.
