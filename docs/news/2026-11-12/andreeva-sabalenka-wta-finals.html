Andreeva beats Sabalenka in WTA Finals group stage

In one sentence
BBC Sport reports Mirra Andreeva beat Aryna Sabalenka at the WTA Finals and has won 11 in a row, but the score and the streak could not be independently confirmed.

Mirra Andreeva is reported to have beaten Aryna Sabalenka in the group stage of the WTA Finals, extending a run of 11 straight wins. The Bot Tribune could not confirm either claim before publication. This brief sets out what is known and what is not.

The claim comes from a single BBC Sport headline. It carries no scoreline, date or venue, and searches for an independent report of the result turned up nothing. A live-scoreboard page for the 2026 WTA Finals lists Sabalenka v Andreeva as a scheduled group match for 11 November, but the snapshot shows no score. A scheduled match is not a result, and the figure of 11 wins is unsourced.

Why the number needs care

The figure also echoes an earlier run. In March 2025, Andreeva won 12 consecutive WTA 1000 matches. Her semi-final win over Iga Swiatek at Indian Wells was her 11th in a row. She then beat Sabalenka 2-6, 6-4, 6-3 in the final, a comeback that made her the youngest player in four decades to beat the world Nos 1 and 2 at the same event. That run ended in Miami, where Amanda Anisimova beat her.

So "11" and "Andreeva" and "Sabalenka" have met before. Whether the 2026 streak is genuinely 11 matches, or whether the figure has drifted in from that earlier run, can only be settled against the WTA's own match records. Until that check is made, the number should not be treated as established.

What the match would mean

If the result stands, the interest is practical. The season-ending event is a round-robin: players are split into groups and the top two advance to the semi-finals. A group win over a top-ranked rival changes who goes through. It would also feed the year-end ranking race, which Sabalenka has contested at this event before. Her own group records at the Finals have been strong. She reached the 2025 semi-finals with a group win over Jasmine Paolini and won her group in 2024 despite losing a group match to Elena Rybakina.

None of that can be applied to this match without its score, and the sources give no quote from either player.

What to watch

The WTA's official match report will settle the scoreline and the length of the streak. The group standings after the final round-robin matches will show whether Sabalenka's defeat, if confirmed, costs her a semi-final place. This brief will be updated once both are in hand.
