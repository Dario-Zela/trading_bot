Tottenham v Arsenal: north London derby could shape both seasons

In one sentence
Today's BBC One north London derby is the Women's Super League meeting, and the men's table shows Arsenal second and Tottenham 16th, so no mutual season-defining stakes there.

The north London derby on BBC One this afternoon is the women's one. The men's sides have no league meeting today, and the table says why that matters. The premise that this fixture will "shape both seasons" holds up poorly for the men.

Tottenham Hotspur Women host Arsenal Women in the Women's Super League at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, kick-off 12.45pm BST. Coverage is on BBC One, BBC iPlayer and the BBC Sport website, according to club and listings sources. Pheobe Cross referees.

What the tables say

On the men's side, the numbers undercut any talk of a mutual season-definer. As of today, Arsenal are second in the Premier League on 18 points from seven games, three behind unbeaten Manchester City on 21, according to a table published by Swiss outlet nau.ch. Tottenham are 16th on 6 points from seven, with six goals scored and ten conceded. That is a gap of 12 points. It is also a long way from a contest between rivals chasing the same prize.

The women's picture is closer, though not by much. Spurs are fourth in the WSL, five points off the Champions League places, per a preview from the fan site To The Lane And Back. That preview did not give Arsenal's position or points, and a full WSL table was not independently checked for this piece, so how much separates the sides is not established here.

The women's derby

Spurs started the season well. Manager Martin Ho won the WSL Manager of the Month award for September, but two disappointing results this month have slowed them. Ho has been keen to damp expectations: asked about a top-four finish, he said "we are not at that level". Captain Drew Spence has said the team must fix "a few things" before the derby.

Team news favours neither side. Jess Naz is in contention to return for Spurs after an ACL injury that has kept her out since December 2025, while Olga Ahtinen misses out with a small knock. Arsenal remain without Michelle Agyemang and Katie Reid, both recovering from long-term ACL injuries, and Selia Cerci and Mariona Caldentey are also absent.

What to watch

For Spurs, the arithmetic is simple. A win would pull them toward the Champions League places they are five points short of, while a second poor result in a few weeks would make Ho's caution look prescient. For Arsenal, the absentees test squad depth early in the season. The men's teams do not meet in the league today. Their next fixtures will show whether Tottenham, 16th and three points above the bottom of the table's relegation-side cluster, are looking up at the derby or down at the drop.
