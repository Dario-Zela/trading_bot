Bayern boss Kompany taken to hospital after collapsing at training

In one sentence
Bayern Munich manager Vincent Kompany was taken to hospital after collapsing at training; he is reported stable, with no diagnosis and his staff covering matches for now.

Bayern Munich manager Vincent Kompany was taken to hospital on Friday after collapsing at the club's training ground, a few days before the Bundesliga leaders were due at Freiburg.

The club said the 40-year-old was watching a session involving the reserve team when he collapsed. German broadcaster Sportschau, citing BR24Sport, reported that his condition is stable. Bayern said he is undergoing a thorough examination and will stay under medical care. The club has not given a diagnosis.

What is known

Kompany had been unwell for about a week with a viral infection. He said he was on the mend after Bayern's Champions League draw with Arsenal on Wednesday, two days before the collapse. Whether the illness and the collapse are connected has not been established, and the club has not said so.

Bayern have taken him out of match operations for now. His coaching staff will run training and matchday duties in the meantime. Sporting director Max Eberl said Kompany would be given as much time as he needs, which suggests the club is not working to a return date.

The reports come from the club's statement as relayed by the BBC, RTÉ, Al Jazeera, ABC and the Washington Post, plus Sportschau. No outlet has yet carried a medical bulletin beyond the word "stable".

What is not

The gaps matter more than the facts. There is no diagnosis, no timetable for his discharge and no word on who will stand in the dugout at Freiburg on Sunday. Bayern have not said whether an assistant will formally take charge or the staff will share the load.

That last point is the one with sporting consequences. Kompany has run a tightly organised side since arriving in 2024, and a few days of disruption in an otherwise settled season is manageable. A longer absence would be a different matter, and Bayern would then have to name an interim figure and say how long the arrangement lasts.

What to watch

The first marker is the next club update, which should say whether Kompany has been discharged and whether the viral infection played a part. The second is the team sheet and touchline arrangements for Freiburg on Sunday, which will show who is in charge in his absence. Until then, this is a health scare with a reassuring early word and little else.
