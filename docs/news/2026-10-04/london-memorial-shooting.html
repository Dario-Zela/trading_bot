Man shot dead at London memorial service

In one sentence
A 40-year-old man was shot dead in East Acton, west London, as around 50 people including children gathered for a memorial, and the Met has opened a murder inquiry.

A 40-year-old man was shot dead in west London on Friday evening as dozens of people, including children, gathered for a memorial service. The Metropolitan Police have opened a murder investigation. No arrests had been made by Saturday, and a cordon remained in place at the scene.

What happened

Officers were called to East Acton just before 7.50pm on Friday after members of the public reported hearing shots fired. The incident was near the junction of Braybrook Street and Erconwald Street. Police say between 40 and 50 people were there at the time, gathered in remembrance of someone who had died.

Paramedics from the London Ambulance Service attended alongside police. The man was pronounced dead at the scene despite what the force called the "best efforts" of medical staff. Police have not publicly named him, and the Met has not said whether he was the target of the attack or what the motive may have been.

The police response

Detective Chief Inspector Brian Howie, who is leading the inquiry, said:

"Our thoughts are with the family and friends of a man whose life was taken in a senseless act of violence. We understand a large number of people had gathered for a memorial service and what should have been a time to come together in remembrance has instead left a family devastated."

The size of the crowd means the investigation is likely to depend heavily on witness accounts. Officers can be expected to canvass those who were present, collect mobile phone footage and review nearby CCTV and doorbell cameras. The Met has not said whether a weapon has been recovered or whether it is looking for a vehicle.

What is not yet known

Several basic questions remain open: the victim's identity, the number of attackers, and whether anyone else was hurt. Police have not described the memorial itself, including whom it was for. Reports so far do not suggest the shooting was linked to terrorism, and the force has treated it as a murder inquiry.

Firearms killings are comparatively rare in Britain, where handgun ownership is tightly restricted, though they do occur in London. A shooting at a gathering of this kind, with children present, is unusual even by that measure, and it leaves a community that came together to mourn with a second loss.

What next

The immediate steps are formal identification of the victim and notification of his next of kin, followed by a post-mortem examination. Police have appealed for anyone who was at the memorial, or who has footage from the area around the time of the shooting, to come forward. Any arrests, and the release of the man's name, will be the next signs of how far the inquiry has progressed.
