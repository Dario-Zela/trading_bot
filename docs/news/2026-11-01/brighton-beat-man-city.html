Brighton win at Manchester City for the first time in the league

In one sentence
Yasin Ayari's 11th-minute goal gave Brighton a 1-0 win at Manchester City, their first league victory at the Etihad, leaving City top but beaten twice running.

Brighton & Hove Albion beat Manchester City 1-0 at the Etihad Stadium on Saturday. It was their first league win at City's ground, in their 18th attempt. It also left the league leaders with back-to-back defeats.

The goal came early. In the 11th minute Matheus Nunes miscued a clearance and Yasin Ayari took the chance from close range, beating Gianluigi Donnarumma. Ayari had missed a penalty against City in the EFL Cup three days earlier, so the finish also settled a personal score.

City's pressure, Brighton's resistance

City spent the rest of the afternoon chasing the game. They had 21 shots and did not score, which ended a run of 24 home league matches with a goal. Bart Verbruggen made six saves for Brighton, including a fine one to deny Nunes, and tipped an effort onto his own crossbar. Erling Haaland was starved of service for long periods.

Brighton also had chances to extend the lead. Iliman Ndiaye headed narrowly wide in the first half and hit the post. Fabian Hürzeler's side defended in numbers and counted on the single goal, and it was enough.

The result follows Brighton's midweek Carabao Cup meeting with City at the Etihad. Brighton twice took the lead in that game before City went through on penalties. This time there was no shootout.

What it does to the table

The title framing needs a caveat. City still top the Premier League with 21 points. Arsenal are level on 21 with a game in hand, and Brighton are third on 19 points from nine matches. City's Saturday defeat came after a 2-1 loss to Aston Villa, so the lead has not gone. It is thinner than it was, and Enzo Maresca's side now have two league defeats in a row to explain.

One caution on the record. The 'first away league win' claim comes from the Premier League's own social media post and from match reports. Brighton had beaten City in the league before, most notably in May 2021, but not at this ground.

What next

City need to stop a two-match slide before Arsenal, who have a match in hand, move clear. Brighton, third and two points off the top, will want to show that a result like this is part of a pattern rather than an exception.
