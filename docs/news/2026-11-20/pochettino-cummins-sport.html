SPORT · MANAGERS AND CAPTAINS · By K. Brennan · 2026-11-20 · 3 min read

Pochettino's reported Old Trafford priorities; Cummins' captaincy questioned

In one sentence
Reports list culture, defence and Fernandes as Pochettino's first jobs at Old Trafford, but the agenda is analysis rather than confirmed club policy, and Cummins' captaincy has no verified trigger.

Mauricio Pochettino's name is again attached to the Manchester United job, and with it a tidy agenda: fix the culture, tighten the defence and settle the Bruno Fernandes question. The list is neat. Its provenance is less so. The triage source for this piece is a single BBC Sport item that could not be checked directly, and the coverage found since is mostly preview and opinion rather than club statements.

What is reported

Several outlets frame Pochettino's arrival as imminent or done, and they broadly agree on the in-tray. Culture comes first. One analysis argues that Pochettino, PSG aside, has a record as a culture-builder, which Erik ten Hag and Ruben Amorim were meant to be and were not. Another says people at United want football played "the United way", with young players brought through and a push for silverware.

Defence is second. Reports point to costly individual errors, goalkeeper Senne Lammens among them, and Fox Sports lists more disciplined defending among four priorities.

The third is Fernandes. The captain is described as having been United's most consistent performer in recent seasons, but his form has dipped this term. One report says he has turned down a contract extension. The suggestion is that Pochettino would need him on side, leading by example at 32.

Treat all of this as analysis. None of the pieces found quotes a named club source on record, and the account of the appointment itself varies between them. A manager's "first priorities" are, in practice, a columnist's reading of an obvious set of problems. Fernandes, Harry Maguire and Luke Shaw reportedly hold contract options that could run their deals by a year, and a January transfer budget is also mentioned. Both are unconfirmed.

The captaincy thread

The other angle in this brief is Pat Cummins. He has captained Australia's Test side since late 2021, and the next Ashes series is in England in 2027. No verified trigger has turned up for a captaincy debate: no selection decision, injury bulletin or board move.

What exists is background. Head coach Andrew McDonald said in December 2025, when Cummins' back was a problem, that there was "no finality or timeline on Patty". Steve Smith has stood in six times. A speculative squad piece from January named Cummins captain for 2027, but that was one pundit's picks, not a selection.

What ties them together

Both stories ask whether a captain fits the project around him. Fernandes is the armband holder a new manager must either build around or work past. Cummins is the presumed leader whose fitness decides how much Smith leads. Neither has a confirmed development yet.

The things to watch are specific. For United, an official club announcement and Pochettino's first press conference would show which priorities he names himself. For Australia, Cummins' fitness record through the home summer and any word from Cricket Australia's selectors will settle whether the question is real.
