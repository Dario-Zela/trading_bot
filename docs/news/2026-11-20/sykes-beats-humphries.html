Sykes stuns Humphries at the Grand Slam of Darts

In one sentence
An unseeded Sykes has beaten Luke Humphries at the Grand Slam of Darts in a reported thriller, though the score and round are unconfirmed.

An unseeded Sykes has beaten Luke Humphries at the Grand Slam of Darts in Wolverhampton, in a match reported as a thriller. It is a sizeable result for the draw, though not a seismic one.

The score, the round and the margin could not be independently confirmed at the time of filing. The only report of the result is the BBC Sport item that flagged it, and a search of the PDC and broadcaster results pages turned up nothing on the match. Those details are left out here rather than guessed at.

Why it counts for something

Humphries is a former world champion and a fixture near the top of the rankings. He was runner-up to Luke Littler in last year's Grand Slam final, which Littler won 16-11. He is also the sort of player the format is meant to test, with group darts and a short route to the knockouts. Losing to an unseeded opponent in that setting is the kind of result the tournament exists to produce.

The Grand Slam begins with a group stage, so one defeat does not end a campaign. Whether this one is damaging depends on the group table. If Humphries still has a route through on leg difference or a later fixture, this is a bruise. If it leaves him needing a favour, it becomes a problem. The reports available did not settle that.

What to watch

The next data point is the group standings. Humphries' remaining fixtures and Sykes' own next match will show whether this was a one-off or the start of a run. An unseeded player who takes a top name early often finds the draw more open than expected, and a group winner takes a more favourable path into the knockout rounds.

Until the standings are confirmed, the fair reading is a moderate upset: notable for darts followers, a good night for Sykes, and not yet a change to the title picture. The tournament runs to 22 November, and Littler remains the man to beat.
