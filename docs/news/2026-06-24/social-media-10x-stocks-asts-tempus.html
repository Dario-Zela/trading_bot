Social media's latest 10x list: AST SpaceMobile at four, Tempus AI at seven

AST SpaceMobile's BlueWalker 3 prototype — precursor to the BlueBird commercial constellation now in orbit — photographed from the ground in 2023. — M. Tzukran / International Astronomical Union / CC BY 4.0
In one sentence
Insider Monkey's social-media 10x list puts AST SpaceMobile at #4 and Tempus AI at #7 — a sentiment read, not a fundamental one.

Every few months, the internet rediscovers that speculative stocks are fun. Insider Monkey's latest contribution to the genre is a listicle titled "10 Stocks That Will 10X According to Social Media," ranking equities by how frequently and seriously they were discussed on Reddit, X, and Stocktwits. AST SpaceMobile (NASDAQ: ASTS) landed at number four; Tempus AI (NASDAQ: TEM) at seven. The editors were upfront about the methodology: conviction in the posts was what they were tracking, not evidence for it.

What the hype is buying

For AST SpaceMobile, the social media narrative is not hard to locate. The company is building a direct-to-device cellular broadband network in low Earth orbit, partnering with AT&T, Verizon, and Vodafone to deliver satellite-backed coverage to ordinary smartphones. In early June it launched BlueBird satellites 8, 9, and 10 on a SpaceX Falcon 9. The vision — blanket connectivity without a specialist handset — addresses a genuinely large market.

The stock has already behaved like something the crowd believes in, hard. ASTS hit an all-time high of $133.86 on 28 May before shedding more than 40 percent over the following weeks, trading near $73 by late June. Thirty-nine hedge funds hold the stock, which gives it some institutional scaffolding, but the underlying numbers are demanding. Q1 revenue came in at $14.7 million, missing estimates by nearly $22 million, while operational cash burn runs at roughly $48 million per quarter on top of $262 million in quarterly capital expenditure for new satellite hardware. Insider Monkey's headline — "Retail Investors Say Ignore SpaceX Hype and Buy This Space Stock With 10X Potential" — frames a competitor risk without running the arithmetic on the company's own.

Tempus AI, ranked two spots lower, occupies somewhat firmer ground. The company's diagnostic testing and data platform recorded $348 million in Q1 2026 revenue, up 36 percent year-on-year, and is guiding for $1.59 to $1.60 billion for the full year. Its 126 percent net revenue retention in 2025 suggests clients are expanding their spend rather than churning out. AstraZeneca, GSK, and Merck have each signed contracts valued up to $320 million. Thirty-three hedge funds track the name. None of that explains, however, why Reddit's 10x thesis is or is not warranted at current prices — TEM has already retreated from a 52-week high near $104 to around $48, and Insider Monkey itself concludes the publication prefers other AI names with greater near-term upside.

The value of this kind of list is not zero. High social-mention frequency in speculative sectors can presage unusual volume or options positioning, and aggregating retail conviction is a legitimate data point. What it does not supply is signal on timing, magnitude, or probability. For ASTS specifically, the next hard marker is Q2 revenue — due in August — which will reveal whether the expanding BlueBird constellation is translating satellite capacity into a commercial ramp the stock once priced at three digits.
