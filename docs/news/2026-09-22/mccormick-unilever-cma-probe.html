McCormick's $44.8bn Unilever foods deal faces UK competition probe

In one sentence
The CMA has formally opened a Phase 1 inquiry into McCormick's takeover of Unilever food brands including Knorr and Hellmann's, with a decision due November 11.

The CMA has formally opened a Phase 1 inquiry into McCormick's takeover of Unilever food brands including Knorr and Hellmann's, with a decision due November 11.

Deal execution risk is now material for both companies' shareholders and signals regulatory willingness to scrutinize large consolidation in consumer staples. A blocked or heavily-conditioned deal would reshape McCormick's growth strategy and could cool M&A appetite in the sector.

Key facts:
- $44.8bn acquisition of Unilever's food brands (Knorr, Hellmann's, others)
- CMA formally opened Phase 1 investigation; decision deadline Nov 11, 2026 (~7 weeks)
- Brands under review hold significant share in UK condiments and bouillon categories
