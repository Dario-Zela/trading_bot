Lewis left out of England's Rugby League World Cup opener

In one sentence
Hull KR half-back Mikey Lewis is a non-playing reserve for England's World Cup opener against Tonga in Perth, with Williams and Smith picked at half-back.

England's Rugby League World Cup campaign will open without Mikey Lewis in the matchday 17. The Hull KR half-back is among the non-playing reserves for Saturday's game against Tonga in Perth. Lewis only just made the wider squad, so the omission is a setback rather than an exclusion from the tournament.

The selection

Brian McDermott has paired captain George Williams of Warrington with Wigan's Harry Smith at half-back. Lewis joins Ben Currie and Ben Talty as one of three players who will not play. Lewis is not on the interchange bench either.

There was a back-story. Lewis had earlier edged out Jake Connor for a place in the World Cup squad. Connor was the 2025 Super League Man of Steel, so his absence from the squad was itself a talking point. Having picked Lewis over Connor, McDermott has now picked Williams and Smith over Lewis. The reports paraphrase McDermott as saying Lewis had done enough to be among the half-back options. They also say he wanted heritage players, so as to build the strongest squad available.

The other call of note is at full-back. AJ Brimson gets the shirt ahead of Jack Welsby, who is on the interchange bench.

The team

The reported starting 13 is:

  • AJ Brimson
  • Dom Young
  • Hugo Savala
  • Herbie Farnworth
  • Zach Eckersley
  • George Williams
  • Harry Smith
  • Max King
  • Brad O'Neill
  • Morgan Knowles
  • Junior Nsemba
  • Kai Pearce-Paul
  • Victor Radley

The interchange is Jez Litten, Luke Thompson, Ethan Havard, Sam Walters, Jack Welsby and Matty Ashton.

What it changes

Not much, yet. A reserve in game one is not a reserve for the tournament, and injuries, form and suspensions tend to reshuffle half-back pecking orders quickly. The selection does show the pecking order, though. Williams and Smith have the first call on the halves, and Lewis, who beat the Man of Steel to a squad spot, sits behind them.

The reporting here rests largely on BBC Sport's team announcement as syndicated by other outlets. McDermott's own comments were reported second-hand, with no direct quotes found.

The next test is the Tonga game itself. If England's halves struggle in Perth, the question of Lewis's role will be asked again before the next group fixture.
