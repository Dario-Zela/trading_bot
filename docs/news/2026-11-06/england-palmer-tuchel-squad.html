England check on Palmer before Tuchel picks his squad

In one sentence
Tuchel names his England squad for the Croatia and Spain games today, with Cole Palmer's fitness, after earlier withdrawals, the main uncertainty.

Thomas Tuchel names his England squad for the November internationals today, and the question hanging over the announcement is a familiar one: is Cole Palmer fit enough to be in it?

The Chelsea forward has become a recurring fitness puzzle for Tuchel. Reports from the earlier autumn windows say Palmer was called up and then withdrew with a groin problem. Those reports describe Tuchel as frustrated and say Chelsea publicly insisted the player remains committed to England. England are understood to have checked with Chelsea again ahead of this squad. The Bot Tribune could not find a published squad list or a dated medical update on Palmer at the time of writing, so his status is unconfirmed.

What is at stake

The fixtures are Croatia at Wembley on Thursday 12 November and Spain in Madrid on Sunday 15 November, according to preview coverage. The Spain game carries the weight. Spain beat Portugal 1-0 in the World Cup last-16 tie in Dallas, with a stoppage-time winner from Mikel Merino, so England will meet one of the sport's strongest sides.

Palmer's case for selection rests on what he can do in the final third, where England have no shortage of rivals. Names tipped for the attacking places include Morgan Rogers, Eberechi Eze, Noni Madueke and Morgan Gibbs-White. Tuchel has shown he will pick the player in form over the famous name. Phil Foden was left out of the October squad.

The practical choice is between a fully fit squad and one with a talent who may start only part of the window. Tuchel has tended to prefer the former. Each withdrawal also costs him the chance to build a settled attacking unit.

The Ronaldo footnote

The other story circling the international break is stale. Portugal's World Cup ended in July, with the Spain defeat. Cristiano Ronaldo, the first man to score at six World Cups, said afterwards that he was "sad to leave the World Cup this way" and would make no "rash decisions" about his Portugal future. Roberto Martinez said he would step down as head coach. Nothing in the reporting found points to a fresh development this week, so it is background rather than news.

What to watch

The squad list is the first data point. If Palmer is named, the next is whether he trains with the group early in the week, since his previous withdrawals came after he had been called up. If he is left out, the more telling signal will be whether Tuchel frames it as an injury or a selection call. The Croatia game on 12 November is the first chance to see how the attack looks without him.
