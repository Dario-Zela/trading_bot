Airbus taps Scaleway for AI and defence cloud in European sovereignty push

In one sentence
Airbus signed a multi-year deal with Iliad's Scaleway to host sensitive industrial and defence AI workloads, a vote of confidence in homegrown cloud over US hyperscalers.

Airbus signed a multi-year deal with Iliad's Scaleway to host sensitive industrial and defence AI workloads, a vote of confidence in homegrown cloud over US hyperscalers.

This is a market signal—not just an isolated contract. Major defense spending (Airbus is a €60B+ revenue enterprise) migrating to European infrastructure reflects accelerating geopolitical pressure on US cloud dominance in regulated sectors. It validates the emerging sovereign-cloud market and could drive valuations of European tech players while pressuring US majors' growth assumptions in Europe.

Key facts:
- Airbus signed a multi-year cloud infrastructure agreement with Scaleway (owned by French firm Iliad) for sensitive industrial and defence applications.
- The deal explicitly supports deployment of AI tools and is positioned as part of a European sovereignty push.
- Scaleway is a European alternative to dominant US cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), competing in the emerging 'sovereign tech' market segment.
