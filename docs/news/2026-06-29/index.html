For trading firms in particular, the implication is stark. Competitive advantage no longer flows from algorithm alone. Instead, access to compute infrastructure has become the measurable edge: firms with secure data-center capacity can deploy and iterate on trading strategies faster than rivals facing cooling or power bottlenecks. The result will likely be a widening performance gap between infrastructure-rich and infrastructure-constrained players.
