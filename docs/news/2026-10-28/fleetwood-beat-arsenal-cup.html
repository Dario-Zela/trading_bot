Fleetwood knock Arsenal out of the cup; Dowman sent off

In one sentence
Fleetwood beat Arsenal 5-3 on penalties after a 1-1 draw, with Max Dowman sent off and Mikel Arteta seen berating him on the touchline.

Fleetwood Town, of League Two, knocked Arsenal out of the Carabao Cup on Tuesday night, winning a fourth-round shootout 5-3 after a 1-1 draw at Highbury. Arsenal's night ended in the 85th minute, when the 16-year-old Max Dowman was sent off. A touchline exchange with his manager then drew as much attention as the result.

The night

Fleetwood took the lead through Aaron Loupalo-Bi, who finished smartly in the first half. Arsenal drew level through Viktor Gyökeres, five minutes after the interval. Reports differ on the exact minutes, and no source consulted gives a fuller account of the 90 minutes. Neither side found a winner, and the tie went to penalties.

There, the goalkeeper Jay Lynch saved Bruno Guimarães' kick, and Conor Haughey scored the decisive penalty for the hosts. Fleetwood's manager, Matt Lawlor, put the night in perspective, as reported by BBC Sport: "The biggest night in the club's history in terms of opposition coming to town and we've managed to beat the champions."

The tie turned on the dismissal. Dowman, who has been one of the season's more talked-about teenagers, appeared to kick out at the Fleetwood midfielder Harrison Neal after a robust challenge. Goal describes it as a reactionary kick, and one outlet called the decision harsh. A straight red for violent conduct normally carries a three-match domestic ban, though no ban has been confirmed. Arsenal then had to see out extra time and penalties with ten men.

Arteta's touchline moment

The cameras followed Dowman off the pitch, and Mikel Arteta was seen berating him on the touchline. Goal described the Arsenal manager as animatedly berating the teenager, and Arsenal Insider said it was unclear what was said but that Arteta did not look happy.

Arteta spoke to BBC Radio 5 Live about it. He said the exchange concerned "something that we discussed already before the match." On the incident itself, he said: "It's difficult to see on the cameras like that." Another outlet reported that he praised his players' attitude despite the defeat, and he congratulated Fleetwood.

The angle that Arteta's conduct has been "questioned" needs care. In the coverage reviewed, no named critic, pundit or governing body has criticised him. The questions come from the pictures of a manager publicly upbraiding a 16-year-old, and from the lack of detail about what "already discussed" means. There is no report of an FA charge against Arteta or Arsenal.

What next

The immediate question is Dowman's disciplinary outcome. An automatic ban would rule him out of the next few league and cup games, including a trip to Liverpool, according to one report. Whether Arsenal appeal is not yet known.

The larger question is how Arteta handles a teenager he has leaned on heavily this season. For Fleetwood, the result is a defining one. Whether the club is rewarded with a glamour draw in the quarter-finals is the next thing to watch.
