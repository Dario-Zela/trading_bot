Man City in advanced talks for Liverpool's Cody Gakpo

Cody Gakpo in action for Liverpool, the subject of Manchester City's £85m deadline-day pursuit. — Wikimedia Commons
In one sentence
Manchester City are closing in on an £85m deal for Liverpool's Cody Gakpo, with personal terms discussed as the transfer window shuts Tuesday.

Cody Gakpo scored Liverpool's equaliser-that-wasn't-quite on Saturday, teeing up Alexander Isak for a simple tap-in against Nottingham Forest, then trooped off midway through the second half and watched his side draw 2-2. By Sunday afternoon he was the subject of an £85m tug-of-war between Liverpool and Manchester City, with the transfer window slamming shut in under 48 hours.

City have opened advanced talks over a deal worth more than £85m ($115m) and have been encouraged that Gakpo is receptive to a move to the Etihad, with personal terms already under discussion between the player's camp and the club. Liverpool, who had spent most of the summer insisting the Dutch international was not for sale, are now understood to be prepared to let him go — but only once they have banked his replacement.

A rebuild chasing a rebuild

The irony is thick. Manchester City are in the market for Gakpo because they spent the summer dismantling their own attack: Savio departed for Tottenham in a club-record £75m deal, Omar Marmoush followed him to north London for around £60m, and further down the spine Rodri left for Barcelona, Bernardo Silva and John Stones exited on free transfers, and Tijjani Reijnders moved to Saudi Arabia's Al-Qadsiah. It is the heaviest turnover at the Etihad since Pep Guardiola arrived a decade ago — fitting, perhaps, since Guardiola isn't the one picking the team anymore. Enzo Maresca, poached from Chelsea in the close season, is overseeing the reconstruction, and has been candid about the gaps left behind. "This is a squad that doesn't need to do many things," he said last week. "At the same time, the club lost important players in terms of experience — Bernardo, John, Nathan, different players. So for sure there are things that we need to do." City are also chasing Crystal Palace's Ismaila Sarr as a second wide option, but Gakpo, a proven Premier League goal threat with 18 goals and six assists in his first season under previous manager Arne Slot, is the priority.

Liverpool's own reshaping under new head coach Andoni Iraola — the Spaniard who replaced Slot this summer after three seasons turning Bournemouth into European qualifiers — explains why a deal that would have been unthinkable in June is now plausible. Liverpool are on the verge of signing Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain for £106m, rising to £123m with add-ons, on a five-year contract, and have already added Victor Munoz from Osasuna, who scored a stunning equaliser of his own against Forest on his Anfield debut. Only once Barcola's move is registered, sources indicate, will Liverpool sanction Gakpo's exit. Asked about his squad's shape with the window closing, Iraola offered little: "Now we have two or three days and we will see what happens with everyone."

What's next

The Premier League's summer window closes at 11pm BST on Tuesday, and both deals — Gakpo out, Barcola in — are racing the same clock, with a completed deal sheet offering clubs a two-hour grace period to finish paperwork if talks run long. Matchday two continues regardless: Chelsea host Brighton, Manchester United face Ipswich Town, and Sunderland meet Fulham, all before deadline day dawns. Whether Gakpo lines up for City this season, or Liverpool decide £85m still isn't enough to sell a direct rival their most in-form forward, should be settled by Tuesday night.
