Jay-Z reported for Super Bowl half-time; Meghan to co-host Junior MasterChef Australia

In one sentence
A BBC line says Jay-Z is the Super Bowl half-time act, but the NFL and Roc Nation have said nothing, and a Meghan MasterChef Junior role is also unconfirmed.

Two entertainment lines turned up on the BBC's feeds today. Neither has the confirmation a reader would expect from a firm announcement.

Jay-Z and the half-time slot

The BBC World line says Jay-Z is the Super Bowl half-time performer. It gives no date, venue or statement from the NFL or from Roc Nation, Jay-Z's company.

The fuller version of the rumour is more hedged than the one-liner. Puck reported that Jay-Z had chosen himself as headliner for Super Bowl LXI, due on 14 February 2027 at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles. Other outlets have repeated that claim and cite Puck. Complex and Pajiba both carry it as a rumour. The reports say Miley Cyrus and Justin Bieber were also considered. Jay-Z has not commented in public.

The idea that he picked himself rests on a real business arrangement. Roc Nation has been the NFL's live-entertainment partner since 2019, and the league renewed the deal in 2024 for five more years. How much say Jay-Z has is disputed. One account describes him as having the final word. Another says the NFL and its producers, Jay-Z among them, draw up a shortlist and do not simply choose.

One detail in the background material did not survive checking. It said Jay-Z played the 2015 half-time show, but that show was headlined by Katy Perry. He has not performed at half-time, and a 2017 report said he turned down the Super Bowl LII slot. A first appearance would be a milestone, which is partly why an unsourced line deserves caution. The claim stands as a report.

Meghan and the junior cooks

The second item is thinner still. The BBC UK line says Meghan will co-host Junior MasterChef Australia. It gives only a first name and does not say who she is.

A search found no confirmation of that role. It did turn up a nearby fact. Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, took part in the adult series of MasterChef Australia this year, on her first visit to the country since 2018. Senal News calls her a guest judge. The Nightly calls her a guest host. Prince Harry reportedly appeared by phone during the episode. Junior MasterChef Australia is a separate spin-off for contestants aged 8 to 12. Nothing found links her to it, and the BBC's "Meghan" may be a different person.

What would settle it

Neither item is ready to be treated as news. The half-time claim needs a statement from the NFL or Roc Nation, with a second outlet independent of Puck. The NFL has usually announced its half-time acts in the autumn, so a formal word could come soon. The Meghan claim needs the broadcaster, Network 10, to say who is joining the junior series and in what role.
