Kane wins Ballon d'Or; Putellas takes record-equalling third women's award

In one sentence
Harry Kane won the men's Ballon d'Or, England's first in 25 years, and Alexia Putellas took a third women's award to equal Aitana Bonmatí's record.

Harry Kane is the Ballon d'Or winner for 2026. He is the first Englishman to take the men's award since Michael Owen in 2001, a gap of 25 years. Alexia Putellas won the women's award for the third time. That ties the record held by Aitana Bonmatí.

The ceremony was held at the London Palladium on Monday night, a nod to Stanley Matthews, who was nominated for the first Ballon d'Or in 1956. It started 45 minutes late because of traffic and congestion getting into the venue.

A 25-year wait, not 70

There is an obvious trap in the arithmetic. Matthews won the first award in 1956, and a reader might assume England has waited since then. It hasn't. Bobby Charlton won in 1966, Kevin Keegan won twice, in 1978 and 1979, and Owen won in 2001 after his Liverpool season. Kane ends the 25-year gap since Owen. He is the fifth Englishman to win.

The numbers behind it are plain. Kane helped Bayern Munich to a 13th Bundesliga title in 14 years and scored six goals for England at the World Cup. He also took the Gerd Müller Trophy for the top scorer. Before this year he had never finished higher than tenth in the vote. Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappé were the other leading names in the race. The reports reviewed here do not give the order of the runners-up.

No quotes from either winner were available in the coverage reviewed.

Putellas equals the record

Putellas first won in 2021 and 2022. She has now added a third award, the same total Bonmatí reached with her wins in 2023, 2024 and 2025. Putellas scored 24 goals with 13 assists in her final season at Barcelona, a campaign that included a quadruple. She has since moved to London City Lionesses.

Barcelona dominated the rest of the women's honours. Pere Romeu was named coach of the year and the club took club of the year. Cata Coll won the Yashin Trophy for the best goalkeeper, and Vicky López won the women's Kopa Trophy for the second time.

The rest of the night

On the men's side, Paris Saint-Germain were named club of the year and Luis de la Fuente, the Spain manager, took coach of the year. Yamal won the Kopa Trophy for the best young player for the third year running, and Unai Simón of Athletic Bilbao won the Yashin Trophy. Arsenal's Bukayo Saka won the Socrates Award.

The next question is whether Putellas can pass Bonmatí. That will not be settled until the 2027 ceremony.
