Isak scores twice as Liverpool beat Club Brugge

In one sentence
Alexander Isak scored in the 13th and 82nd minutes, with Florian Wirtz also on target, as Liverpool won 3-0 at Club Brugge in the Champions League.

Alexander Isak scored twice as Liverpool won 3-0 at Club Brugge on Wednesday night. It was a comfortable Champions League result, and it gave the striker a night that his season has not often supplied.

The match was played on 25 November. Isak put Liverpool ahead in the 13th minute, punishing a loose pass from the Brugge goalkeeper, Sommer, according to Sky Sports and 101 Great Goals. After that the home side created little. One Lemarechal header, which went too close to Alisson, was the pick of their chances.

A late flurry

The game was decided in a short spell near the end. Florian Wirtz made it 2-0 in the 79th minute, and Isak added his second three minutes later, in the 82nd. For the first hour or so Liverpool were ahead by a single goal and Brugge were in the game. Two goals in four minutes ended that.

The second goal was also a good one for Wirtz, who has had his own difficulties settling in at Anfield. Liverpool's match report credited both players, headlining the win as one in which Isak and Wirtz "strike".

What it says about Isak

The case for a striker rediscovering his form rests on thin evidence, and it should be stated as such. Isak's record at Liverpool has been uneven. A December 2025 piece in the search record put his Champions League return at two goals in 14 appearances for the club at that point. That figure predates this season's matches and is not a current tally. A brace, one of the goals from a goalkeeper's error and the other with the game already largely settled, is encouraging rather than conclusive.

It is still the kind of night a forward needs. Isak has had two goals in a game before, in a Premier League win at Ipswich, but a European away fixture is a different test, even against a Brugge side that offered little resistance.

What's next

Liverpool's next test is the rest of the Champions League league phase, where the points from this win help their position in the table. Reports differ on exactly where it leaves them. Isak's next opportunity to show that this was more than one good evening comes in Liverpool's next league fixture.
