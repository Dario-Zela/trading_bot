Lammens howler denies Man Utd victory against Sporting in Pochettino's first match

In one sentence
Pochettino's second match in charge — not his first — ended 2-2 at Sporting after Lammens spilled a cross for Suarez's 80th-minute equaliser, leaving United still without a win under him.

Manchester United's search for a first win under Mauricio Pochettino goes on. A 2-2 draw at Sporting in the Champions League on Tuesday night was thrown away in the 80th minute, when goalkeeper Senne Lammens spilled a cross and Luis Suarez hooked in the equaliser.

The result is usually described as Pochettino's first match in charge. That isn't quite right. It was his second. His first, at Liverpool on Sunday, ended in a 4-0 defeat. Pochettino has now taken charge of two games and has conceded two penalties and had two players sent off. The draw is a better result than Anfield, but it still does not feel like progress.

A match of two goalkeepers' moments

Sporting were awarded a penalty early on, and Lammens saved Suarez's effort in the 31st minute. Seventy-five seconds later he came off his line and clattered Maxi Araujo, and the second penalty was given. Suarez scored it in the 33rd minute.

United were denied an equaliser shortly before half-time. Amad Diallo put the ball in the net, but the referee had already blown for a foul by Youri Tielemans. Sky Sports said Tielemans had won the ball cleanly with a slide tackle, and the whistle meant VAR could not step in. Pochettino called the decision difficult to understand.

Bruno Fernandes levelled it after the break. He converted a penalty in the 48th minute, won when Eduardo Quaresma brought down Lisandro Martinez, then scored again two minutes later. The second was a low shot from distance that bounced off an uneven patch of turf and into the net. It was a soft goal, and United took it.

The howler, and the goal that wasn't

United held the lead until the 80th minute. A cross dropped into the box, Lammens failed to claim it, and the ball fell to Suarez. He struck it into the net with an acrobatic finish.

United thought they had a late reply. Patrick Dorgu's contact with the goalkeeper, Kaique, led to a corner that Kaique punched into his own net in the 84th minute. After a VAR check, the goal was disallowed. Jamie Carragher called the decision "very harsh." Tielemans was then sent off in stoppage time for a second yellow card.

Pochettino was upbeat afterwards. "I'm so proud of the players. It was a tough game," he said. Fernandes was more candid about the goalkeeper. "Mistakes are part of football and from that you get goals," he said, adding that the squad "need to really stick together and understanding that we need to fight for our lives."

What it means

The sporting cost of one dropped point in the league phase is modest. The managerial cost is harder to measure. Pochettino is two matches into a job he took after United activated his buyout clause earlier this month, and he has inherited a club 19th in the Premier League. Lammens, whose earlier error against Tottenham in October also cost points, is becoming a recurring subject. Two spilled balls and a foul are not a trend on their own, but United do not have much margin.

The next test is at home to Brentford on Saturday. It offers Pochettino a chance to get his first win at Old Trafford, and Lammens a chance to put a bad night behind him.
