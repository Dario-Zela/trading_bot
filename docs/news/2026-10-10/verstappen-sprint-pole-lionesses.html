Verstappen takes sprint pole after another Red Bull settings issue; Lionesses win in Greece

In one sentence
Verstappen has sprint pole after another Red Bull settings problem, while England's women won the first leg of their World Cup play-off in Greece.

Max Verstappen has taken sprint pole, but the more telling detail is how Red Bull got there: after another settings problem with the car. For a team that has spent the season chasing a stable setup, getting the car into the right window at all is the story.

A familiar pattern

The Dutchman's pole follows the latest in a run of setup troubles at sprint weekends. Earlier this season in Canada, Verstappen complained over team radio that his RB22 was jumping around in sprint qualifying, and he started seventh. Red Bull has since repeatedly described its fixes as adjustments rather than cures.

Sprint weekends make that harder to hide. There is a single practice session before qualifying, so a car that arrives in the wrong window has little time to be corrected. Pole here shows the fix worked on the day. It does not show the underlying fault has gone.

The details of this weekend's settings issue were not available to verify at the time of writing, and neither were the margin of pole or the cause. The reliability reading rests on the pattern across the season, not on a confirmed diagnosis of this one.

Lionesses take the first leg

In football, England's women won the first leg of their World Cup play-off against Greece, played on Friday in Greece. The final score was not confirmed in the material available.

The tie matters because England missed automatic qualification for the 2027 World Cup in Brazil. A 4-0 defeat by Spain in June let Spain top the group and sent Sarina Wiegman's side into two rounds of play-offs. It was also the first senior meeting between the two nations' women's teams.

The return leg is in England on Tuesday 13 October. Progress would set up a tie against the winner of Slovakia v Ukraine, in the international window from 26 November to 5 December, with the Round 2 winners going to Brazil.

What next

For Red Bull, the test is the grand prix itself. A sprint pole earned with a car that needed repairs will count for little if the settings drift again once the long runs begin. For England, the second leg against Greece on Tuesday is the next box to tick.
