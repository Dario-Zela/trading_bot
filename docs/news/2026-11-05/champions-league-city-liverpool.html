Haaland winner sees City past Leipzig; Liverpool lose in Istanbul

In one sentence
Haaland's 78th-minute goal gave Manchester City a 3-2 win at Leipzig, while Liverpool lost 3-2 at Fenerbahçe after twice leading, ending their unbeaten Champions League start.

Manchester City won at RB Leipzig 3-2 on Wednesday night, with Erling Haaland scoring the decider in the 78th minute. In the same round of Champions League fixtures, Liverpool lost 3-2 in Istanbul, beaten by Fenerbahçe after twice taking the lead. Both games finished 3-2, and both turned on a late goal.

Leipzig twice pull level

City went ahead in the 37th minute, when Castello Lukeba put the ball into his own net. Leipzig levelled through Ridle Baku in the 49th minute. Enzo Fernández put City back in front in the 58th, and Antonio Nusa made it 2-2 three minutes later.

Haaland settled it with 12 minutes left. He finished from close range at the back post after a cross from Elliot Anderson. It was his 13th goal in nine appearances against Leipzig, more than he has scored against any other opponent. Most of that tally came in the 7-0 round-of-16 second leg in 2023, when he scored five.

City had the better of the chances by expected goals, 2.23 to 0.87, although Leipzig had more shots, 14 to 12. The result lifts City into the top eight of the 36-team league phase. Leipzig are tied for 29th on three points, inside the elimination zone.

Istanbul again

Liverpool arrived unbeaten in the competition. Trey Nyoni and Alexander Isak each gave them the lead at Fenerbahçe, and each lead lasted about four minutes. Mason Greenwood equalised in the 29th minute and Archie Brown in the 73rd. Vedat Muriqi scored the winner in the 79th.

Andoni Iraola called the goals Liverpool conceded "cheap" and "messy". They came from a free-kick, a corner and a long throw-in. Reports of the match point to a breakdown in communication between Cody Gakpo and goalkeeper Alisson. Fenerbahçe were led by the interim coach Dirk Kuyt, a former Liverpool player, who was in charge for only the second time after Ismail Kartal left the club last week. Fenerbahçe moved up to 19th in the table.

The result is another poor night for Liverpool in Istanbul. They lost 1-0 to Galatasaray there in September 2025, and again in the first leg of the last-16 tie in March 2026. The trip to the city is a long way from the 2005 final, which Liverpool won from 3-0 down.

The pre-match material for this piece mentioned a goalkeeper named Owono. No match report found for the game names him, and the goalkeeper in the accounts of Liverpool's defeat is Alisson.

What it changes

The table is why the Leipzig result counts for more than one winning goal. Eight sides are guaranteed direct entry to the last 16, and 9th to 24th go into a play-off round. A win away from home against a side who fought back twice keeps City near the top. Leipzig now face a run of fixtures in which dropping more points would put them out of the top 24 and into elimination.

Liverpool's position is less pressing. They remain among the leaders after an unbeaten start, but the defeat ends that run and leaves their top-eight place less secure. The set-piece lapses Iraola identified are the obvious thing to fix before the next matchday.
