Analysts flag rarest bitcoin bottom signal in 15 years for August 31

In one sentence
A rare Bitcoin/stocks ratio pattern is due to confirm August 31, but it's based on just three prior instances and other analysts still see a lower bottom first.

Bitcoin is trading around $64,000, down roughly half from its October 2025 peak of $126,000, and for the first time in 14 years it is losing ground to the S&P 500 on a rolling basis. Against that backdrop, one chart pattern has crypto traders circling August 31 on their calendars — the date a technical setup last seen at the 2022 bottom is due to complete.

In one sentence: A rare Bitcoin/stocks ratio pattern flagged by one analyst is due to confirm August 31, but it rests on just three prior instances and other forecasters see a much lower bottom first.

The signal comes from Oliver L. Velez, a veteran technical analyst who tracks the ratio of the S&P 500 level to Bitcoin's dollar price — the inverse of the usual comparison. On a four-month chart, Velez has identified a specific setup: three consecutive "green" bars, each printing a higher high than the one before it. That sequence has appeared only three times in Bitcoin's history, and each time it coincided with a major cycle low.

Three data points, one pattern

The third bar of the sequence peaked on January 14, 2015, with bitcoin near $171; on December 7, 2018, near $3,381, eight days before that cycle's actual bottom; and on November 22, 2022, near $16,197. A fourth sequence began forming in December 2025, added a second bar in April 2026, and its third bar — already above the prior bar's high — closes on August 31, 2026.

That is a three-for-three record, which is the entire basis for calling it rare. It is also a sample size of three, built on a ratio and a bar interval Velez chose himself, with no independent backtesting published alongside it. Velez has said as much: the pattern, in his own words, "offers no guaranteed outcome." A signal that has fired three times in fifteen years is not yet a signal with a track record — it is an interesting coincidence that has not been wrong.

A different backdrop this time

The macro picture around this fourth signal also looks less favorable than its predecessors. Spot bitcoin ETFs logged $5.4 billion in net outflows in the first half of 2026, their first negative half-year since launch, and the S&P 500-to-bitcoin ratio broke above its 200-week moving average for the first time since 2012 — investors have been rotating out of crypto and into AI and mega-cap tech stocks rather than chasing bitcoin. Other analysts reading the same market disagree sharply on timing: Markus Thielen of 10x Research puts a floor near $55,000 with a bottoming window stretching from August into October, while BitMEX's Arthur Hayes has argued bitcoin could still fall toward $40,000 before any turn.

None of that invalidates Velez's chart. It does mean August 31 is a checkpoint for one indicator among several disagreeing ones, not a settled date. If the pattern holds to form, the next test is whether price actually stabilizes in the weeks after the bar closes, the way it did — narrowly — in 2018. If it does not, the fourth instance will be the first time in the pattern's short history that it failed.
