Fleetwood to face Everton or Newcastle in the League Cup quarter-final

In one sentence
League Two Fleetwood, who beat Arsenal on penalties, will host the winner of Thursday's Everton v Newcastle tie in the League Cup quarter-finals.

Fleetwood Town, a League Two club, have reached the League Cup quarter-finals for the first time in their history. They did it by knocking out the reigning Premier League champions, Arsenal. The draw has now been made, and the opponent is still open. Fleetwood will play the winner of Everton against Newcastle United, which is the last fourth-round tie and is due to be played at Hill Dickinson Stadium on Thursday.

The triage note described Fleetwood as a League One side. That is wrong: the club is in League Two, which makes the draw a bigger story than a routine lower-league cup tie.

The upset that made the draw

Arsenal went to Highbury Stadium on Tuesday night and left without winning. Aaron Loupalo-Bi put Fleetwood ahead in the 21st minute. Viktor Gyökeres levelled early in the second half. The tie then went to penalties, and Fleetwood won the shootout 5-3, scoring all five of their kicks. Conor Haughey took the decisive one.

The result is the kind League Cup nights are remembered for. A fourth-tier side took down the champions, and the neutral gets a shock to talk about. Arsenal, for their part, were left to reflect on a bad night. Mikel Arteta described the exit as a "very sad day" in a headline quoted by one outlet, though the full remarks were not available to check.

The draw

The quarter-final ties, as drawn, are:

  • Fleetwood Town v Everton or Newcastle United
  • Manchester City v Bradford City
  • Crystal Palace v Sunderland
  • Liverpool v Bournemouth

Fleetwood have been drawn at home, so the winner of the Merseyside-or-Tyneside tie will travel to Highbury Stadium. That is a neat reward for a club that has just beaten Arsenal there. Bradford City, another lower-league side, will visit Manchester City.

The either/or wording on Fleetwood's tie is not stale information. The draw was made before the last fourth-round game had been played, so one pairing was left open by design.

What comes next

Everton and Newcastle settle the matter on Thursday. The quarter-finals are due in the week commencing 14 December, with some reports suggesting 15 to 17 December. The exact date and kick-off times have not been confirmed.

Whoever comes through will arrive at a ground that has just seen the champions beaten. Fleetwood will have six weeks or so to prepare and to enjoy it.
