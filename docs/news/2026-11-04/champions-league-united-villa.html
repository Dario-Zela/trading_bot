Man Utd come from behind to beat Roma; Villa fall short against Barcelona

In one sentence
Man Utd came from 2-0 down to beat Roma 3-2, easing some pressure on Carrick, while Aston Villa lost 3-0 at Barcelona.

Manchester United came from two goals down to beat Roma 3-2 at Old Trafford in the Champions League on Tuesday night. It was a result that gave Michael Carrick some relief after a bad run. Aston Villa had a harder evening in Spain, beaten 3-0 by Barcelona.

United's second-half turnaround

Roma were ahead before the interval. Matías Soulé gave them an early lead and Donyell Malen doubled it from the penalty spot. Old Trafford had seen this kind of night before, and the mood soured.

The second half changed that. Bruno Fernandes pulled one back in the 58th minute from a retaken penalty. Matheus Cunha then scored twice in quick succession to finish the comeback. Reports on the night describe the finish as late, so exact timings should be checked against the official match record.

The win matters mostly for what it interrupts. Several reports say it ended a nine-game winless run in all competitions. Carrick had said in the build-up that he expected to remain in charge, after a 2-0 Premier League defeat at Chelsea that deepened the pressure on him. One comeback does not settle a manager's future. It does remove the most immediate argument against him, and it may buy some patience.

The evidence is thin on what comes next. No club statement or board comment on Carrick's position has been reported alongside the result. The case for him now rests on whether United can follow a good night with a good week.

Villa meet a different level

Aston Villa had no such recovery at Camp Nou. Lamine Yamal opened the scoring and Raphinha added two goals after the break, giving Barcelona a 3-0 win. Reports say it lifted Barcelona into second place in the league phase table.

The scoreline reads as a clean gap, and a 3-0 defeat away to one of Europe's strongest sides is not shameful. It is useful as a measure, though. Villa have shown they can compete in this competition. On this evidence, competing with Barcelona's front line over 90 minutes is a different test.

What next

The two results point in opposite directions. United have a performance to build on and a manager with a little more room. Villa have a reminder of the distance to the top of the table, with the rest of the league phase still to play. The next signal for Carrick is United's next league fixture, where the question is whether the Roma second half was the start of something or a one-off.
