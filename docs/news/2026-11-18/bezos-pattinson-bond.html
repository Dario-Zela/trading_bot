Bezos says he was 'heavily involved' in casting Pattinson as Bond

In one sentence
Jeff Bezos says he was 'heavily involved' in casting Robert Pattinson as Bond, a single-source claim that tests how far Amazon now steers the franchise's creative choices.

Robert Pattinson was named as the next James Bond on Monday, and the owner of the studio that hired him now says he had a hand in it. Jeff Bezos has said he was "heavily involved" in the choice, according to BBC Business. The exact wording is as relayed by the BBC; it has not been independently checked here.

The remark is easy to treat as celebrity trivia. It is more useful as evidence of how far a technology owner reaches into a film franchise with 60 years of British history behind it.

The announcement

Amazon MGM Studios confirmed Pattinson on 16 November as the seventh actor to play 007, succeeding Daniel Craig. Press reports put the film under the direction of Denis Villeneuve, and say Pattinson has signed for multiple films. Filming is reportedly aimed at spring 2027, with a release in late 2028. Those details come from entertainment coverage rather than from a full studio breakdown, and should be read with that in mind.

The casting itself was not a surprise. The Hollywood trades had reported for months that Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult had both tested for the part. Accounts from earlier in the process, all anonymously sourced, said audition footage was flown to the US so that Bezos and Amazon's film chief Mike Hopkins could watch it, and that Bezos wanted the actor to be British.

Why the claim matters

Amazon bought MGM, which co-owns the Bond franchise with Eon Productions, in a deal announced in 2021 at about $8.45bn and completed in March 2022. For three years, Eon's Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson kept creative control. In February 2025 that changed, and Amazon took over creative control of the franchise. Around the same time, Bezos posted on X asking his followers: "Who'd you pick as the next Bond?"

That post was a public sign of interest. A claim of heavy involvement in casting goes further, because casting the lead is the single most consequential creative decision a Bond film makes. If Bezos personally weighed in, the franchise's creative direction is now being set at the top of a company whose main business is selling everything else.

Two caveats apply:

  • Single source. The triage rests on one BBC Business report. Neither Amazon MGM nor Eon has put out a statement describing Bezos's role, and a founder's own account of his influence is not the same as a studio's.
  • Owner involvement is normal. Studio heads and owners routinely sign off on a franchise lead. What is unusual is that the person doing so is a billionaire who publicly polled his followers beforehand.

What to watch

The test is whether the casting is a one-off or a pattern. The next decisions are the supporting cast, the release strategy (cinemas first or streaming on Prime Video) and how much of the franchise's tone survives its new owner. The first marker will be any on-the-record statement from Amazon MGM Studios about who approved what, ahead of the start of filming next spring.
