King marks 78th birthday with radio show, joking with Elton John

In one sentence
King Charles joked with Sir Elton John about ageing on a special Rocket Hour for The King's Trust's 50th anniversary, broadcast to mark his 78th birthday.

King Charles III turned 78 on Saturday, and marked the day by doing what many people do when a birthday arrives: he had a laugh at his own expense, in the company of an old friend. The friend was Sir Elton John, and the occasion was a special edition of John's long-running radio programme.

The episode was a one-off of Rocket Hour, the weekly show John has presented on Apple Music 1 since 2015. It was recorded to celebrate 50 years of The King's Trust, the youth charity Charles founded as the Prince's Trust in 1976, and was first broadcast on Saturday to coincide with the birthday, according to ABC News coverage of the clip.

'Still standing'

The exchange that has travelled furthest is a short one. The pair got onto the subject of John's hit I'm Still Standing, which, as reports note, Charles had heard performed live at a 1986 concert. The King remarked that the two of them were still standing. John replied that he was "still sitting now, though", which drew a laugh from the King.

Charles then offered a line of his own, saying that "occasional bits" were dropping off, "a terrible problem as we get older".

It is a small moment, but it fits a pattern. The King has long been comfortable with self-deprecating humour, and he and John have appeared together before, most recently at the Royal Variety Performance in November 2024, where the King was photographed beaming as he greeted the singer.

A second turn behind the microphone

The appearance is not the King's first venture into radio. In March 2025 he presented a one-off Commonwealth Day programme on Apple Music 1, built around fifteen of his favourite artists from across the Commonwealth. Diana Ross and Michael Bublé were among the names on that playlist. Apple Music, in other words, has become something of a regular route for the palace to reach a younger and more international audience than a traditional broadcast slot would.

The birthday itself is a fixed point: Charles was born on 14 November 1948 and became king on 8 September 2022, after the death of Queen Elizabeth II.

What to watch

The King's Trust anniversary runs on past this weekend, and further events around the 50-year mark are the natural place for the charity to extend the attention this clip has drawn. The full Rocket Hour episode is on Apple Music for anyone who wants more than the clip.
