Alcaraz beats Shelton to win first Paris Masters title

In one sentence
Carlos Alcaraz beat Ben Shelton 7-6(5), 7-5 to win his first Paris Masters title, avenging a US Open quarter-final loss to the American.

Carlos Alcaraz won the Paris Masters for the first time on Sunday. He beat Ben Shelton 7-6(5), 7-5 in the final at La Défense Arena. It was a measured answer to the defeat Shelton handed him two months ago in New York.

A first in Paris

The title is Alcaraz's fifth of 2026 and his ninth at Masters 1000 level, according to Yahoo Sports and the Associated Press. It extends his run to nine straight match wins. The indoor hard court in Paris had been one of the few gaps in his collection, and he had not reached the final in earlier attempts. Last year he went out in the second round to Cameron Norrie.

The match took just under two hours. Alcaraz saved six break points in the opening set, which Shelton's serve kept tight. The tie-break went to the Spaniard 7-5. In the second set he dropped serve but broke straight back, then held on to close it out 7-5.

The rematch

The Shelton meeting carried a back-story. The American beat Alcaraz in a late-night, five-set quarter-final at the US Open in September, finishing it with a final-set tie-break. The earlier loss was in a Grand Slam quarter-final, not a final. Sunday's result makes it four wins in five meetings for Alcaraz.

France 24 described Shelton as error-prone on the day, and the break points he failed to convert in the first set were the difference. For Shelton, it was a first Paris Masters final and a strong week: he beat Tommy Paul in the quarter-finals and reached his first semi-final in the French capital.

What comes next

Paris is the last Masters event of the season. The ATP Finals in Turin follow, from 15 November, and Alcaraz arrives with form and momentum. The question there is whether he can carry nine straight wins into a round-robin format that gives little room for a slow start.
