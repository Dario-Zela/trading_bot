Government launches independent review of pub and hotel business rates

In one sentence
An independent review led by Jerry Schurder will examine whether business rate valuations unfairly burden pubs and hotels, reporting to the Treasury by March 2027.

Signals potential policy tailoring for a politically important sector, but the timeline is long and the outcome uncertain. Traders tracking hospitality stocks and UK property valuations should note this as a medium-term policy risk.

Key facts:
- Independent review led by Jerry Schurder will assess whether business rate valuations unfairly burden pubs and hotels
- Review will report to the UK Treasury by March 2027
- Business rates are a major operating cost for UK hospitality; rate changes could materially affect sector margins
