Tearful McIlroy dedicates record-equalling win to ill father

In one sentence
McIlroy finished second in Dubai but won a record-equalling eighth Race to Dubai title, matching Colin Montgomerie, and dedicated it in tears to his father, who is recovering from a double lung transplant.

Rory McIlroy fought back tears in Dubai on Sunday as he secured a record-equalling eighth Race to Dubai title and dedicated it to his father, Gerry, who is still recovering in hospital after a double lung transplant.

McIlroy did not win the tournament itself. Tyrrell Hatton took the DP World Tour Championship at Jumeirah Golf Estates. McIlroy finished solo second on 10 under par, after a closing 67. That was enough to win the season-long money list. It also drew him level with Colin Montgomerie, who won the order of merit eight times.

The record

McIlroy finished one place ahead of Eugenio Chacarra, who led the standings going into the final event and ended two strokes behind him, tied for third. The title is McIlroy's fifth in a row. Alongside Montgomerie, he now shares the most in the history of the European circuit, which is now branded the DP World Tour.

The record is significant in its own right, though it is a season-long prize rather than a single tournament. It rewards consistency over a whole year, which is the quality Montgomerie was known for in the 1990s. McIlroy has said the title "felt different" from his recent wins, and that he had to work hard for it.

"Very proud to win this again, and yeah, put my name alongside Monty's with eight."

The dedication

The emotion came from what McIlroy said about his father. Gerry McIlroy had a double lung transplant in September. He has spent about two months in hospital, including many days in intensive care. McIlroy said the operation was a success and that his father is recovering steadily.

Before McIlroy left for the Middle East, Gerry gave him an instruction: "win me The Race to Dubai." McIlroy said that stayed with him through the week. Reports quoted him saying, with his voice breaking, "This one is for him."

"My dad — hopefully everyone's dad in this room — is a massive part of my life, and has been my biggest supporter."

Gerry has been a fixture of McIlroy's career since the beginning. He worked extra jobs to fund his son's early golf. McIlroy has paid tribute to both parents after his biggest wins, including his second Masters in April.

What comes next

The immediate question is Gerry's recovery. McIlroy's season is over, and he can go home to his father. On the course, a ninth Race to Dubai title would put him alone at the top. The 2027 season is the next chance to win it.

Details here are drawn from ESPN and Yahoo Sports reports of the final round. Some accounts differ slightly on the length of Gerry McIlroy's hospital stay.
