HEALTH · SCREENING · By Dr. P. Aaronson

NHS launches world's largest trial of AI breast cancer screening

The NHS is launching what is described as the world's largest trial of artificial intelligence in breast cancer screening. The EDITH trial was first announced by the government in February 2025; the BBC's report of the launch is dated 23 November 2026.[^1]

The trial asks whether AI can find cancers that current screening misses without producing more false alarms. Its second question matters as much: whether AI can ease pressure on radiologists, because a shortage of trained readers is the main constraint on screening capacity. Sweden's MASAI trial, published in The Lancet Oncology in 2023, reported about 20% more cancers detected and about 44% less screen-reading workload than standard double reading.[^2]

Those Swedish results come from a different health system and do not transfer automatically. Outcome data are years away, so EDITH will first report on accuracy and workload, and only later on diagnoses. Watch for the published protocol and recruitment figures.
