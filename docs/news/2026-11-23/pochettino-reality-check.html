Pochettino faces a chaotic week and a reality check

In one sentence
Pochettino's final week as US coach brought three wins, not a crisis, and the live story is who replaces him after the search that began this week.

The pitch for this piece was a manager in trouble: a rough start for Mauricio Pochettino, a chaotic week, a reality check. The reporting doesn't support that. The week that has just finished was his last in charge of the United States, and it went well on the pitch.

The week that was

Pochettino's final international window ended with three wins. His side beat Uruguay 5-1 in a friendly, with an alternate XI in which Christian Pulisic and Weston McKennie were left out of the squad. He then took the Concacaf Nations League quarter-final against Haiti 6-0 in the first leg on 14 November and 2-0 in the second, in Cincinnati on 17 November. That made it 8-0 on aggregate and booked a place in the March finals. Julian Hall scored in the second leg. It was his fifth goal in six appearances, according to the reports.

That is not a rough spell. It is a clean exit, and the reports describe Haiti as the manager's sign-off.

What is actually at stake

The real story is the vacancy. US Soccer has said its search for a new men's head coach will begin when this window closes. Steve Cherundolo will lead the next camp, which includes a match against Jamaica on 13 December. The questions that matter are who gets the job and how much of Pochettino's selection approach survives. His rotation drew pushback this month, and he said in response that some players are not guaranteed places.

The pitch also names no team, match or scoreline for any slump, and the BBC Sport source gave no text to check. This brief therefore holds back from analysis of a difficult start, because the facts available point the other way. A piece on the succession, once US Soccer names candidates or a timetable, would have an event behind it. This one does not.
