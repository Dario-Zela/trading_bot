NFL week 11: 49ers win in Mexico City as Bills stay top

In one sentence
The 49ers won in Mexico City and the Bills stay top after NFL Week 11, but the source gave no scores or records, so neither result can yet be detailed.

The San Francisco 49ers won their Week 11 game in Mexico City, and the Buffalo Bills remain at the top of the table. That is the extent of what the reporting behind this brief supports. The source, BBC Sport, gave no score, margin, opponent or record, and a search for independent confirmation turned up no verified 2026 box score. Anything more specific would be guesswork, so none is offered.

What is known

The 49ers' win is the headline result. The game was played at altitude in the Mexican capital, which is the novelty of the weekend. The NFL has staged regular-season games in the city since 2016, and the occasion tends to draw a crowd that leans heavily towards the visitors on the day and towards the league's Mexican fanbase more broadly.

The Bills, described as staying top, are the other half of the story. The brief does not say which table that refers to, whether the AFC as a whole or the AFC East. It also gives no record and no tiebreak detail. Those are the details that decide whether "top" means a clear lead or a one-game cushion that could vanish next week.

A precedent, with a caveat

The last time San Francisco played a Week 11 game in Mexico City was in 2022, when they beat the Arizona Cardinals 38-10 at the Estadio Azteca on Monday Night Football. Jimmy Garoppolo threw four touchdowns that night. That is a different season, a different roster and, for all this brief establishes, a different opponent. It is offered only to show that the venue has been kind to the 49ers before, not to describe this week's game.

What to watch

The next data point is the official standings after the Week 11 games are fully logged, which will show whether Buffalo's position is as comfortable as the brief implies. The 49ers' result matters most for the NFC playoff picture, where a single win can reorder the wildcard places. Neither can be judged from the material available here. A fuller account will follow once scores and records are confirmed against NFL.com or the league's official standings.
