Forest stun City as Arsenal stretch their lead; Leeds beat Spurs and Newcastle drop points

In one sentence
Forest beat Manchester City 3-1 with a Delap double, City's third straight league loss, while Arsenal's 1-0 win over Hull left them six points clear.

Nottingham Forest beat Manchester City 3-1 at the City Ground on Saturday. It was City's third straight league defeat, and Arsenal, who won 1-0 against Hull, are now six points clear at the top.

The result at the City Ground

Forest were in front early. Jair Cunha scored in the sixth minute and Liam Delap made it 2-0 by the 18th. Rayan Cherki pulled one back for City in the 52nd minute, and Erling Haaland went close to an equaliser. Delap then added his second in the 73rd to settle it. Goal times come from Hoofoot's listing, and the scorers and score are consistent across ESPN, Sky Sports and City's own match report.

Delap joined from Chelsea in August in a club-record deal reported at up to £50m. This was a big return on that fee. ESPN reports it as Forest's first home league win of the season, and Sky says it lifted them out of the relegation zone. For a club that had gone without a home win, that mattered more than the opponent's name.

For City, the manner of the defeat was as worrying as the scoreline. Enzo Maresca said he was "concerned" about the team's form. Three straight league losses is a run, not a blip, and it answers the question of whether a slow start has turned into a slump. Whether the problem is tactical or about the squad is not something one match report can settle.

Arsenal stretch away

Arsenal gained the most. They beat Hull 1-0, which Sky Sports reports as leaving them six points clear. The win was narrow and unspectacular, but a six-point gap is large this early. It also leaves City, last season's runners-up to the defending champions in the order set out earlier this season, with ground to make up. The points-gap figure comes from reports of the weekend; the league table itself was not independently checked.

Leeds win, Newcastle drop points

Lower down, Leeds beat Tottenham 1-0 at home, according to ESPN and CBS. BBC Sport's headline framed it as the end of a five-game winless run for Leeds. Leeds beat Newcastle 4-1 in September to climb to third, so a return to winning ways was in keeping with how they began the season.

Newcastle went to Fulham and drew 1-1. The BBC's headline credits a Muniz strike with denying them the win, though the minute and the manager's reaction were not available. Matthias Jaissle's side recovered from that 4-1 defeat at Leeds to beat Hull 2-1 on 19 September. A draw at Fulham is a dropped two points rather than a crisis.

What next

City now have to stop the slide before it widens a gap that is already six points. Forest, meanwhile, have the first home win they had been waiting for, and a striker who has started to repay his fee. Sunday's fixtures, including Crystal Palace against Liverpool and Manchester United against Aston Villa, will show whether anyone else can close on Arsenal.
