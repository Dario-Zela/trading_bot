Russia and Ukraine back US-brokered trilateral talks as missiles hit Kyiv

Putin and Zelenskyy both back resuming US-brokered trilateral talks, possibly in Abu Dhabi around 10 November, but strikes on Kyiv and unchanged demands leave it unclear whether this is more than a pause.

The Gulf angle matters. Abu Dhabi's role as a possible host would extend the mediation network beyond Washington and European capitals, and a venue there would signal that the UAE is being trusted with a channel that Western governments have struggled to open on their own. The stakes for Western aid, sanctions relief and the terms of any eventual ceasefire all depend on whether the format survives the next few weeks. Watch for a confirmed date and delegation list, and for whether either side pauses or escalates strikes in the run-up.
