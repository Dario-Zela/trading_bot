Svitolina ends Sabalenka's season at the WTA Finals

In one sentence
Svitolina beat Sabalenka 6-3, 3-6, 6-1 in a decisive round-robin match at the WTA Finals, ending the world number two's season and reaching the semi-finals.

Elina Svitolina beat Aryna Sabalenka 6-3, 3-6, 6-1 at the WTA Finals on Friday, a result that ended Sabalenka's season and sent the Ukrainian into the semi-finals. The match was a round-robin decider, not a knockout tie. Sabalenka needed the win to advance and did not get it.

One detail in the original tip needs correcting. Sabalenka is not the world number one. She lost the top spot to Elena Rybakina in September, ending a 99-week run, and she went into the week as number two. Wikipedia's points table for the event has her second on 6,485 points behind Rybakina on 8,099. It also lists Rybakina as withdrawn from the tournament through injury.

A match of errors

Svitolina took the first set, lost the second, then closed out the third 6-1. She needed four match points to finish it. Yahoo Sports reported that both players made more unforced errors than winners, which fits a scoreline that swung so sharply between sets.

"I was just trying to play one point at a time," Svitolina said afterwards.

She also described the event as "the tournament where you bring everything to the table."

The tournament is being played at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden in California. It was moved there from Riyadh, where the 2025 edition was held, after the Saudi hosting arrangement ended early. One report attributes the move to safety concerns linked to the Iran war.

How the group fell

Sabalenka finished 1-2 in Group 2. She beat Karolina Muchova in her opener, then lost to Mirra Andreeva and to Svitolina. Andreeva topped the group with a perfect 3-0, which included a 6-4, 6-0 win over Svitolina. Svitolina's second-place finish was enough to go through.

For Svitolina, who won the title in 2018, it is a return to the semi-finals for the first time since 2019. The head-to-head still favours Sabalenka 6-2, and the two had met at the Australian Open in January, where Sabalenka won.

Sabalenka's year was strong by most measures and short of her own standard. She won four titles, including Indian Wells and Miami, and reached two major finals, the Australian Open and the US Open. She lost both, and finished without a Grand Slam trophy. She was also beaten by Rybakina in last year's WTA Finals final.

What it changes

The loss changes little in the rankings. Sabalenka cannot add points this season, so she cannot reclaim number one before the year ends. That follows from the schedule rather than from any published projection, and the year-end standings should be checked once the tournament finishes.

The semi-finals come next, with the final on Sunday. Reports differ on Svitolina's semi-final opponent, so that is best confirmed from the official draw.
