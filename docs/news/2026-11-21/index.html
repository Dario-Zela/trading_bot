MARKETS · LENDING · By M. Halloway

Should AI be judging our creditworthiness?

UK lenders are using AI to decide credit, under rules written for human underwriters. There is no AI-specific statute: oversight falls to existing regulators, including the FCA. Its Consumer Duty, in force for open products since 31 July 2023, requires firms to test whether retail customers get good outcomes. The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 eased limits on automated decisions but kept the right to human review. Proxy variables can disadvantage applicants without intent, and the Equality Act 2010 covers indirect discrimination. Watch who is accountable when a model declines someone.
