Arsenal goalkeeper Raya to have scan on injury

In one sentence
Arsenal keeper David Raya has a scan on his left shoulder after going off at Newcastle, with the result due Sunday and a Dortmund–City–Real Madrid run ahead.

Arsenal will know on Sunday how badly David Raya is hurt. The first-choice goalkeeper is to have a scan on his left shoulder after leaving the field in the 2-2 draw at Newcastle United on Saturday, and the club has said nothing yet about how long he might be out.

Raya, 31, walked off holding his left arm and was replaced by Kepa Arrizabalaga. BBC Sport reported that the problem appeared to come from an innocuous challenge with Newcastle forward William Osula. There was no dramatic collision, which tends to make injuries harder to read from the touchline.

Arteta's reading

Mikel Arteta was guarded after the match. "Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is not good news," the manager said. He admitted the extent of the injury was unclear. The scan is the point at which that changes: it should show whether this is a short-term knock or something that costs weeks.

Nothing has been published on the type of damage, a timeline or a return date. Any estimate of an absence at this stage is guesswork, and this article does not offer one.

Why the gloves matter

Raya has played every minute of Arsenal's league and Champions League football this season. Last season he started 37 of 38 league games. Arsenal have, in effect, not had to think about their goalkeeper for well over a year, which is an unusual luxury and an easy one to take for granted.

Alan Shearer put it bluntly on BBC's Match of the Day. "The one player that Arsenal cannot afford to lose is David Raya," the former England forward said. It is a strong claim about a squad with title ambitions and plenty of other quality, but it reflects how settled the position has been.

Kepa would be the obvious replacement. His role has been that of understudy, and he has not been asked to carry a run of high-stakes fixtures.

The calendar

The timing is awkward. Over the next four weeks Arsenal face Borussia Dortmund, Manchester City, Brentford, Tottenham and Real Madrid. That is two Champions League ties, a derby and a meeting with a direct title rival. If Raya is out for any of it, the goalkeeper's absence would be tested early and against strong opposition.

A short absence, a match or two, would be manageable. A longer one would force Arsenal to decide how much to trust the reserve in the games where margins are thinnest.

The scan result is due Sunday, and Arteta's next team news will say whether Raya is available for the Dortmund game.

Sources differ on Raya's age; BBC and others say 31, one outlet says 29.
