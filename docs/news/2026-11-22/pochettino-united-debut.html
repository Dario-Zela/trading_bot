Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager

In one sentence
Mauricio Pochettino's first game as Manchester United manager is reportedly an away league match at Liverpool, with the result not yet known and some details still to be confirmed.

Mauricio Pochettino takes charge of Manchester United for the first time on Sunday, and the fixture could hardly be harder: an away Premier League game at Anfield against Liverpool. Search results indicate no United manager has had a first league match against Liverpool before. The result was not available when this was written.

The appointment

United confirmed Pochettino as head coach in the middle of last week. He reached the Carrington training ground on Wednesday, 19 November, leaving him roughly four days to put his stamp on a squad he had not worked with. Wire reports of his arrival described a new era beginning with little sleep, and a manager already asking for patience. Their headline phrase was that he wants people to "give me time".

He comes from the US men's national team, whom he has coached since 2024. A Concacaf Nations League quarter-final reportedly delayed his move. Before that came five years at Tottenham, from 2014 to 2019, where he built a reputation for pressing, youth development and overachievement. Those are background details rather than anything reported this week.

The test

Pundits have framed the game as a stern examination rather than a fair one. Anfield is a ground where visiting sides have often struggled. A new manager has had almost no training time and has no obvious way to rework a side's habits in a few days. The realistic aim is probably organisation, intensity and a few clear instructions, not a finished tactical identity.

One caution applies to previews of this kind. A first match tells little about a manager. Several of Pochettino's earlier debuts, and many United debuts since Sir Alex Ferguson, have been poor guides to what followed. Planet Football and Empire of the Kop have both run features ranking those debuts, and the lesson in both is that a debut is not a verdict.

What comes next

The schedule eases a little afterwards. Reports put his first home game at Old Trafford against Brentford on 28 November. That is the first match where he can claim a full week of preparation, and the one against which his early work will be judged more fairly.

A caveat on sourcing. Details of the appointment and fixture come from secondary reports and a web search, not from the club or the league directly. They should be checked against BBC Sport and the official fixture list before the piece is relied on. The score and any post-match comments from Pochettino are not yet in hand.
