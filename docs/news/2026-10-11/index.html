RUSSIA SANCTIONS · By C. Tanaka

US suspends Russian diesel sanctions to April 2027 as Kyiv calls it a 'gift to Putin'

The US has licensed Russian diesel imports until 7 April 2027 to ease pump prices before the midterms, drawing Zelensky's charge of a 'gift to Putin' and raising doubts over its own sanctions law.

Freight and farm operators, who feel diesel prices most directly, are the other audience.

Watch for the exact licence terms, any extension or narrowing of the April 2027 date, and whether European and other partners follow Washington or push back.

WORLD · POLICING · By C. Tanaka

Bid to overturn the ruling that shields police from negligence claims

A new legal challenge asks the courts to reopen a long-standing rule that shields police from negligence claims over how they investigate crime. The protection dates to Hill v Chief Constable of West Yorkshire, in which the House of Lords held in 1989 that police owe no common-law duty of care to individuals in investigating crime. In 2008, the House of Lords extended that protection to negligence claims arising from failed investigations, including claims that police failed to prevent harm. If the rule were reopened, victims of investigative failings could sue for damages, shifting costs onto forces and the Home Office. The claimant, court and hearing date are not yet confirmed, so the timing of any hearing or ruling is what to watch.
