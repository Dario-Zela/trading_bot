Mbeumo offers Carrick solace as United's winless run goes on

In one sentence
A 1-1 draw at Leeds, with Mbeumo scoring again, leaves United with one league win from seven under Carrick and the squad-or-manager question unresolved.

Manchester United went to Elland Road on Sunday looking for a way out of a slump and left with a point, a goal for Bryan Mbeumo and no clearer answer on whether the problem is the squad or the man picking it. Leeds 1, United 1 leaves Michael Carrick's side with one win from seven Premier League matches (four draws, two defeats), by Sky Sports' count.

A lead that did not last

Mbeumo put United ahead in the 35th minute, finishing low past James Trafford from a Youri Tielemans cross. It was his fourth goal of the season, according to reports of the match. The lead lasted until the 51st minute, when Tarik Muharemovic knocked down a corner and Dominic Calvert-Lewin scored from close range. BBC Sport's player ratings gave Mbeumo a 6, saying he "eventually got his goal" but was not strong enough to hold off Calvert-Lewin for the equaliser.

The pattern is familiar. Eight days earlier, Mbeumo had given United the lead against Tottenham in the 73rd minute, finishing from a Matheus Cunha cross. Dominic Solanke equalised in stoppage time, even though Tottenham had lost Rodrigo Bentancur to a red card. A defeat by Atletico Madrid came in between.

Squad or manager?

Mbeumo is the useful test case. If the squad were the problem, its best attacker would be struggling too. He is not: he scored in both of the last two league games, and Carrick praised his finishing and his versatility after the Spurs draw. The failure is in what happens after the goals. Two leads were thrown away within a few minutes of the restart or the final whistle, which points to game management and shape rather than a shortage of talent.

Carrick's own comments after Leeds were not a ringing endorsement of what came before. "It felt like a bit of a fresh start today, in terms of where we're at," he said, and he spoke of "a freshness" in the change of shape. He also said there "was definitely commitment. It had to be there today." Managers do not usually talk about commitment as a selling point unless it had been questioned.

The counter-argument is that one forward in form cannot carry a side. Before this run, Carrick's tenure began with a 2-0 win over Manchester City in January, with Mbeumo scoring the opener, and Leeds won at Old Trafford in April. The good days have been real, but so have the lapses.

What next

United face a Champions League trip to Como and then the next league fixtures, and the run can only be judged by results. The measure is simple: a first league win since August, and a lead that survives to full time.
