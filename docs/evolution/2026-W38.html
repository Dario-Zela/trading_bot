What we read this week 5 findings

Regime-break detection and point-in-time backtest hygiene dominate this cycle's research; attention-based stat-arb and GP-discovered non-linear factors push signal engineering forward, but most direct value for our slate is methodological, not a new alpha source.

RESEARCH · FITS

Structural breaks in factor premia (regime-dependent timing)

Cui, Feng, Ma & Su (SSRN, 2025-2026) fit a characteristic-based factor model with total-variation regularization and find discrete, factor-specific regime shifts that cluster around macro-financial stress episodes; accounting for them improves out-of-sample factor pricing and long-short performance versus static benchmarks. A companion arXiv paper on Bayesian mislearning under structural breaks (2603.21672) shows agents who ignore breaks persistently misprice risk premia during the transition.

Implication. fits existing: macro-aligned — use break-clustering-around-stress-episodes as a trigger to tighten/loosen the regime signal (yield-curve/DXY) rather than treating it as a static filter

RESEARCH · METHODOLOGICAL

Attention/transformer-based conditional factors for stat-arb

Epstein's 'Attention Factors for Statistical Arbitrage' (arXiv:2510.11616) learns latent factors from firm-characteristic embeddings via attention, reporting an out-of-sample Sharpe above 4 gross and 2.3 net of costs on large-cap US equities over 24 years — a meaningful jump over linear characteristic-based factor models.

Implication. out of scope: the model is built as a long-short, cross-sectional stat-arb portfolio requiring shorting and frequent rebalancing across hundreds of names — incompatible with our long-only, ~£10k-per-strategy retail constraint, though the attention-weighted ranking idea could inform mean-reverter's scoring function long-term

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

Genetic-programming-discovered non-linear factors

Chin (SSRN:5369286) applies genetic programming to systematically generate thousands of non-linear combinations of standard characteristics, uncovering anomalies that remain robust out-of-sample versus their linear counterparts, arguing non-linear interactions (not just new data) are an underused source of edge in factor investing.

Implication. spawn-candidate: a GP-evolved non-linear variant of mean-reverter (e.g. non-linear RSI x SMA20-distance interaction term discovered via search rather than hand-set thresholds)

RESEARCH · FITS

Point-in-time universe construction and backtest integrity

Dead Signals Lab (Sept 2026, via Quantocracy) reconstructs historical S&P 500 membership and shows a backtest's net Sharpe collapsed from 0.63 to 0.06 after applying point-in-time index-membership filtering alone — a bigger effect than price/volume choices. This echoes broader 2026 commentary (hedgefundalpha.com backtesting-mistakes guide) that look-ahead bias from using today's constituent list is one of the most common and most damaging backtest errors.

Implication. fits existing: control-rule-based — audit whether its "top previous-day gainers" universe uses point-in-time constituents/tradeable universe at each historical date, since this filter has the largest documented single-line impact on Sharpe seen this cycle

RESEARCH · FITS

Slow diffusion of sentiment into small-cap and positive-news prices

Gambarelli & Muzzioli (Int. Review of Economics & Finance, 2025, Euro-area listed firms 2010-2021) find a statistically and economically significant sentiment-return spread that is priced even after Fama-French controls; under equal-weighting the spread persists up to 3 months but decays within 1 month under value-weighting, and positive news is impounded into prices more slowly than negative news, especially for small caps.

Implication. fits existing: news-reactive — the slow-diffusion-of-positive-news / small-cap-underreaction result suggests widening the news-reactive holding window (toward the 1-5 day upper end) specifically for small/mid-cap UK names rather than treating all tickers uniformly
