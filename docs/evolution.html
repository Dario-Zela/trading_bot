What we read this week 4 findings

Momentum is increasingly seen as calendar- and plumbing-dependent and as decaying hyperbolically; overnight/intraday return reversal is a live topic; LLM news scoring on top of momentum shows modest, look-ahead-sensitive gains.

RESEARCH · FITS

Intramonth timing of momentum returns

Nathan, Suominen and Tasa's 2026 working paper ("The Intramonth Momentum Cycle") reports that US momentum returns concentrate in about six trading days per month, ending roughly four days before month-end. The authors use the May 2024 T+1 settlement change as a natural experiment. I only saw this through a secondary summary (Alpha Architect), not the paper itself, so verify the window and magnitude before acting on it.

Implication. fits existing: momentum-trader (test a calendar gate or position-size tilt around the month-end window; backtest on our own data before trusting it)

RESEARCH · FITS

Factor alpha decay and crowding

Chorok Lee's arXiv:2512.11913 derives a hyperbolic decay form, K/(1+λt), for factor alpha. For momentum it fits better (R²=0.65) than linear (0.51) or exponential (0.61) decay. The listing notes a newer version was withdrawn, so check which version is current. A separate Mozes study (Journal of Beta Investment Strategies, Spring 2026, seen via a secondary summary) links momentum drawdowns to volatility spikes that reverse quickly.

Implication. fits existing: momentum-trader and control-rule-based (add a volatility-spike de-risking rule and track rolling alpha decay per strategy as a retirement signal)

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

Overnight vs intraday return reversal

Lou, Polk and Skouras's May 2026 working paper "The Day Destroys the Night, Night Extends the Day" finds intraday mean reversion to past overnight returns, with overnight and intraday returns related to sentiment in opposite directions. A 2026 Finnish master's thesis (Vainio) reports that large overnight gap-ups partly reverse during the following session. That result is from a long-short strategy; the long-only equivalent is to avoid chasing gap-ups. A New York Fed Liberty Street post (July 2026) also finds the European-open overnight drift was flat in 2021–25, so older overnight effects may have decayed.

Implication. spawn-candidate: gap-fade variant of mean-reverter, buying names that gap down overnight on no news and holding 1-3 days, plus a gap-up filter for control-rule-based. Needs open-price execution checks and realistic UK retail costs.

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

LLM news scoring layered on momentum

Anic, Barbon, Seiz and Zarattini (arXiv:2510.26228) use an LLM to score firm-specific news to condition momentum stock selection. Gains hold in a post-cutoff test period and with 2 bps costs, and are strongest in concentrated portfolios (about 25 stocks). The secondary summaries disagree on the out-of-sample Sharpe (0.57→0.69 vs 0.79→1.06), so read the paper for exact figures. Other 2026 papers (arXiv:2608.12283, arXiv:2605.06730) flag look-ahead and memorisation risk, and one finds sentiment-only gains not significant (p=0.556).

Implication. spawn-candidate: momentum-plus-news-score variant that ranks momentum names by LLM news sentiment from the morning brief, keeping the portfolio concentrated. Evaluate only on data after the model's training cutoff.
