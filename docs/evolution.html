What we read this week 4 findings

Short-horizon reversal is being decomposed (asymmetric, gain-dependent, intraday-driven), LLM news signals are strong in raw form but fragile when bolted onto existing models, and factor-decay and momentum-crash monitoring is moving toward regime gating.

RESEARCH · FITS

Asymmetric and state-dependent short-term reversal

Baldi-Lanfranchi, Collin-Dufresne and Daniel (NBER w35840) report that a factor built on asymmetric idiosyncratic reversal subsumes the idiosyncratic-volatility factor and many other short-horizon anomalies. Cannon and Mohrschladt (JFQA 2026) find the monthly short-term reversal return is 1.25% lower among stocks with the highest capital-gains overhang (CGO) than among those with the lowest, and that the effect is concentrated in illiquid stocks. Barardehi, Bogousslavsky and Muravyev find that past intraday returns show reversal and momentum, while past overnight returns show neither. I did not open the papers' full text, so the figures come from search summaries.

Implication. fits existing: mean-reverter. Test conditioning the RSI<30 entry on the stock's unrealised-gain state and on a liquidity filter. Also test separating intraday-driven drops from overnight gaps, and apply the same overnight/intraday split to the top-gainer chasing in control-rule-based.

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

LLM and text-embedding news signals: strong raw, fragile when bolted on

Didisheim, Kelly, Pourmohammadi and Tian (May 2026, 'The Inefficient Pricing of News') say raw LLM embeddings produce monthly strategies with Sharpe ratios, alphas and turnover comparable to known anomalies. Yerra (arXiv 2605.06730) finds that appending sparse LLM features to a ridge forecaster hurts performance, and that they help only as selective high-conviction tilts. An LLM-scored momentum paper (arXiv 2510.26228, Oct 2025) reports higher Sharpe and Sortino than a long-only momentum benchmark, including after the model's training cutoff. Alpha Architect flags a possible look-ahead issue with after-close news in that paper. The 3.05 long-short Sharpe in Kirtac and Germano (arXiv 2412.19245) is from 2024 and uses a 2010-2023 sample.

Implication. spawn-candidate: LLM-conviction-gated momentum. Take momentum-trader candidates, score each with morning-brief news, and hold only the top few high-conviction names. Enforce strict timestamping so after-close news is not used. Run it as a variant of news-reactive to avoid a new infrastructure build.

RESEARCH · FITS

Factor decay and crowding as a regime signal

arXiv 2611.03668 builds an ML model to predict when cross-factor predictability erodes, reporting a C-index of 0.93 and a regime detector with a 6.6-day delay. The authors frame it as a risk-monitoring signal, and its VIX-tercile regimes are not fully independent of equity returns. Lee (arXiv 2512.11913) fits hyperbolic alpha decay to Fama-French factors, with R² of 0.65 for momentum. It finds crowded momentum has lower crash risk and crowded reversal has higher crash probability. The newer version of that paper has been withdrawn, so only v1 is usable. A Nov 2026 European Financial Management paper on dynamic kernel momentum finds the momentum decay parameter rose after 2000.

Implication. fits existing: macro-aligned. Use a decay or regime-erosion flag to throttle position size in momentum-trader and mean-reverter. Treat the withdrawn paper's crowding result as unverified.

RESEARCH · FITS

Momentum crash risk and regime-gated allocation

A CFA Institute write-up of Baltussen et al. (2026) names crash risk around regime shifts as momentum's main weakness. It suggests volatility scaling and diversified momentum signals as mitigants. arXiv 2604.09060 proposes a momentum-gated optimisation framework, and arXiv 2603.04441 describes a regime-aware strategy that cut equity exposure in the early-2025 selloff. A separate paper using option-implied signals (arXiv 2608.26115) finds crash forecasting works in low-volatility regimes (AUC 0.706) and poorly in high-volatility ones (0.561). The option-implied inputs are out of reach for us, and I did not see out-of-sample results for the other two papers.

Implication. fits existing: momentum-trader and sector-rotator. Add volatility scaling, plus a cash or defensive-ETF switch when a realised-volatility or drawdown regime flag trips. The option-implied crash model is out of scope because it needs options data.
