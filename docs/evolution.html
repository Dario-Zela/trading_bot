What we read this week 5 findings

AI adoption is compressing signal half-lives to ~18 months; regime-conditional routing and industry-relative reversal are the actionable counter-moves for mid-frequency long-only books.

RESEARCH · FITS

AI-Driven Signal Crowding Compresses Momentum Half-Lives

Meng & Chen (arXiv 2605.23905, May 2026) model signal half-life as a function of AI adoption using 99.5 million SEC 13F holdings (2013–2024). At current estimated adoption φ ≈ 0.7, half-lives have compressed from 5–7 years pre-AI to roughly 18 months, and institutional portfolio convergence rose 42% over the sample. The paper derives a 'Red Queen impossibility': as adoption saturates, net alpha across the monoculture approaches zero while market fragility increases.

Implication. fits existing: momentum-trader — the 12-1 cross-sectional lookback window sits squarely in the crowded zone; consider shortening to 3-6 months or overlaying a contrarian exit when rolling-Sharpe drops below threshold

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

Regime-Conditional Strategy Routing via HMM + Cross-Asset Features

A HarbourfrontTechnologies practitioner study (April 2026) combining a Hidden Markov Model regime detector with LightGBM achieves portfolio Sharpe 1.18 (95% CI 0.53–1.84) and a consistent ~17% alpha-positive rate on NASDAQ-100 and S&P 500 under walk-forward evaluation. Key counter-intuitive finding: cross-asset features (Bitcoin, gold/equity flow) provided stronger predictive value than traditional technical indicators, especially for high-beta tech stocks. In bear regimes the model weighted mean-reversion (distance from 200-day SMA); in bull regimes it prioritised market beta.

Implication. spawn-candidate: a regime-dispatch overlay that dynamically allocates capital between mean-reverter and momentum-trader depending on HMM state, using yield-curve + gold/equity ratio as regime inputs (macro-aligned already ingests these)

RESEARCH · FITS

Industry-Relative Reversal: Raw Return Is the Wrong Signal

Gao, Li, Yuan & Zhou (SSRN 6371558, March 2026) show that individual stock one-month reversal is an industry-relative effect — a stock's return minus its industry average predicts next-month return far more powerfully than its raw return. At the stock level, raw short-term returns reverse; at the industry and factor level, short-term momentum persists. This bifurcation holds across multiple formation periods and is not explained by microstructure.

Implication. fits existing: mean-reverter — replace the current RSI <30 + distance-from-SMA20 signal with industry-adjusted return distance (stock return minus sector ETF return) to isolate the clean reversal component and reduce industry noise

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

LLM Top-Down Sector Allocation Outperforms Cross-Momentum Benchmark

Quek Wei Heng et al. (arXiv 2503.09647, ICLR 2025 Workshop) test a three-phase LLM framework (macro assessment → sector weighting → in-sector sentiment stock selection) against cross-momentum on S&P 500 with 10 bp commissions. The LLM allocator achieved Sharpe 2.51 and +8.79% return vs Sharpe -0.61 and -1.39% for the momentum baseline over Jan–Jun 2019. Caveat: only a 6-month backtest; macro inputs are CPI, PMI, FOMC minutes processed via Llama-3.3-70B.

Implication. spawn-candidate: an LLM-enhanced sector-rotator that feeds macro data releases (CPI, NFP, FOMC) into a language model to re-weight sector ETF positions weekly — extends the existing sector-rotator beyond relative strength alone

RESEARCH · FITS

News Sentiment as Drawdown Gate, Not Directional Signal

The ComSIA 2026 hybrid trading paper (arXiv 2601.19504) found that FinBERT sentiment worked best as a protective filter: suspending new entries when average sentiment breached -0.70 reduced maximum drawdown from -19.84% to -15.6% with no degradation in CAGR (53.46%) or Sharpe (1.68), across a 100-stock S&P 500 universe, 24-month backtest. Separately, arXiv 2505.16136 (May 2025) shows FinBERT + XGBoost on GDELT news achieves 72.7% directional hit-rate on FX — but requires leverage and is out of scope for equity-only.

Implication. fits existing: news-reactive — reframe the strategy from directional 'buy on positive news' to a sentiment gate: block new entries (or force exits) when FinBERT morning-brief score is below threshold, reducing drawdown without changing the underlying stock-selection logic
