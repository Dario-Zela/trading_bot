What we read this week 4 findings

Research this week favours regime-conditioned signals. LLM news features help in calm markets and fail in high volatility, factor alpha decays hyperbolically, and overnight-versus-intraday return splits are a live reversal theme. Several findings are secondhand or older than the 4-8 week window, and the brief flags which.

RESEARCH · FITS

Regime-conditional failure of LLM news features

arXiv 2604.10996 tests LLM-derived news features inside RL trading policies. The LLM-augmented agent underperforms the baseline during elevated VIX, and the gap narrows or reverses in low-volatility windows. In the calm H2 2025 holdout, LLM-only features recovered to a Sharpe of about 1.0 against a baseline of 0.809, but no configuration beat the baseline in the H1 2025 validation period. A 2026 review (arXiv 2605.05211) reaches a similar conclusion: sentiment signals are unstable without macro-regime conditioning. I read these results through search summaries, not the full papers.

Implication. fits existing: news-reactive. Gate or size down morning-brief picks when VIX is elevated, and use macro-aligned's regime flag as the gate.

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

LLM-scored news layered on momentum

Anic, Barbon, Seiz and Zarattini (arXiv 2510.26228) use ChatGPT to score firm-specific news for S&P 500 momentum portfolios. They report that the LLM-enhanced long-only momentum strategy beats a standard momentum benchmark on Sharpe and Sortino, both in-sample and out-of-sample. The paper is from late 2025, so it is older than the target window, and I did not verify its costs assumptions or exact figures.

Implication. spawn-candidate: momentum-trader variant that keeps only names with positive LLM-scored news over the last 1-3 days, switched off in high-VIX regimes (per the theme above).

RESEARCH · FITS

Factor alpha decay and crowding

Chorok Lee (arXiv 2512.11913, Dec 2025) models alpha decay as hyperbolic across eight Fama-French factors from 1963 to 2024. The paper reports that crowding-based factor selection failed to generate alpha, with a Sharpe of 0.22 against 0.39 for a simple factor-momentum benchmark. Research Affiliates separately cites a decline of more than 50% in factor performance outside the original sample. Both are older than the target window and I only saw summaries.

Implication. fits existing: sector-rotator and momentum-trader. Add a trailing-performance check that cuts allocation when a signal's recent returns fade, instead of trying to detect crowding.

RESEARCH · SPAWN CANDIDATE

Overnight versus intraday return asymmetry

A Substack post (Harbourfront Quant) cites a 2026 Risks paper by Salotra et al. that tests SPY and nine sector ETFs from 1999 to 2025. It reports that overnight-only long positions consistently outperformed daytime holding. Separate work on overnight-intraday reversal reports that it beats conventional short-term reversal in international equities. I have not verified the paper itself, so treat the finding as unconfirmed. Blitz et al. (2012) add that conventional short-term reversal profits largely vanish after costs, while residual-return reversal holds up better.

Implication. spawn-candidate: mean-reverter variant that sorts on factor-adjusted (residual) returns, or sector-rotator with an overnight-hold leg. Check the cost drag first, since the overnight leg turns over daily at T212 spreads.
